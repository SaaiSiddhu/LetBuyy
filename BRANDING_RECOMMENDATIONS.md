# LetBuyy Branding Recommendations

Generated: 2026-06-03

Scope: recommendations only. No logo, favicon, or brand asset was replaced.

## Logo Scaling

- Keep the logo optically balanced inside the header and sidebar.
- Avoid oversized logo frames that compete with navigation.
- Prefer fixed-height containers and preserve aspect ratio.
- Do not crop the logo unless the image source itself includes excess whitespace.

## Header Usage

- Use the logo as a quiet identity anchor, not a decorative glow element.
- Avoid drop-shadow glow effects on hover.
- Header controls should remain high contrast and compact.
- In light mode, the header should not visually collapse into the sidebar or page canvas.

## Sidebar Usage

- Sidebar should use the separate `#EFEFF1` light surface and `#111827` dark surface.
- Logo placement should align with nav item rhythm.
- The selected nav state should use a subtle neutral background, not a loud brand fill.
- Sales Channels, Apps, and Settings should remain visually separated.

## Favicon Usage

- Confirm favicon legibility at 16px, 32px, and 48px.
- Ensure the favicon works on light browser chrome and dark browser chrome.
- Prefer a simplified mark over detailed wordmark at small sizes.

## Dark Mode Compatibility

- Validate logo contrast on `#111827` and `#0A0F1A`.
- If the current mark loses contrast, create a dark-mode-safe logo asset variant.
- Do not tint the logo purple by default; purple should remain an accent unless the logo system explicitly defines it.

## Recommendation

Keep the current logo assets for now, but remove glow treatment from interaction states in a future brand asset pass. The largest branding improvement will come from consistent placement, sizing, contrast, and favicon clarity, not from replacing the mark during this UI polish wave.
