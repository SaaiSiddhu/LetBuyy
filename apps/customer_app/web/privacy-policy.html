🛡️ Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 11, 2024

Welcome to LetBuyy! Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number when you register
  • Profile Data: Profile picture, delivery addresses
  • Payment Information: Processed securely through Razorpay (we do not store card details)

Device & Usage Information

  • Location Data: With your permission, to show nearby sellers and delivery options
  • Camera Access: For KYC verification and uploading product images
  • Device Information: Device type, OS version, app version for improving our service
  • Usage Analytics: How you interact with the app to improve user experience

2. How We Use Your Information

  • ✅ Process and fulfill your orders
  • ✅ Provide customer support
  • ✅ Send order updates and notifications
  • ✅ Improve our app and services
  • ✅ Verify seller identities through KYC
  • ✅ Personalize your shopping experience

3. Information Sharing

We share your information only in these cases:

  • With Sellers: Order details and delivery address to fulfill orders
  • Service Providers: Payment processing (Razorpay), analytics (Firebase)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect rights

We do NOT sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Secure Firebase infrastructure
  • Regular security audits
  • Access controls and authentication

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Update or correct your information
  • Delete your account and data
  • Opt-out of marketing communications
  • Disable location or camera permissions

6. Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase: Authentication, database, analytics, cloud messaging
  • Razorpay: Payment processing
  • Google Maps: Location services
  • Google ML Kit: Face detection for KYC

Each service has its own privacy policy governing data use.

7. Children's Privacy

LetBuyy is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of changes by posting the new policy on this page.
