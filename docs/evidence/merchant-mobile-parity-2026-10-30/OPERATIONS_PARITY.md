The web dashboard also validates required records and retains independent panel loading, account/store lifetimes and Previous recovery. Summary labels now distinguish catalog products from low-stock location/variant entries.

Account/store changes recreate private panel lifetimes. Late results cannot revive a retired view. Error/denial states clear rows and counts, and a failed later page retains Previous.
