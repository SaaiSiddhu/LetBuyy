# N2.6 — Canonical native billing readback, 15 October 2026

The normal merchant app could reach plan selection but had no navigation to its invoices screen. The dashboard still called a paid Growth store “Starter” through a legacy enum. GET subscription/history returned subscription identities/statuses rather than the existing billing_history receipts; cancellation therefore presented a paid receipt as cancelled. A real Gateway/owned database regression reproduced the wrong invoice ID in run315f6f9008a6 (21 pass,1 expected failure; owned cleanup empty).

## Change

The existing history route now reads only subscription-linked billing_history records. Pending checkout quotes remain excluded; private checkout source fields are not returned. Published SDK amount_paid/status aliases are preserved from invoice amount/payment_status. Receipt identity, number, currency, saved tax/total and period are returned without a second invoice store or client tax calculation. Cancelling the entitlement leaves a paid invoice paid.

GET subscription supplies a fresh scoped product count and the saved active subscription limit. A failed count read is an explicit unavailable response, never a synthetic zero. No subscription/trial limit is invented when the recorded active entitlement is unavailable. This is display convergence; full server entitlement enforcement, trial/grace/renewal/downgrade still require their N2.6 proofs.

Normal Settings now opens Billing and invoices; current-plan and usage subtitles are visible. The dashboard uses canonical billing state instead of legacy plan/commission enums and refreshes after plan management. Read responses are fenced to the current account/store and request version; failed reloads clear old content and offer explicit recovery rather than repeatedly polling. Plan comparison cards show three features; the complete review and accessible card descriptions retain the full feature set. Native invoice money always uses integer minor units; unsupported currency fails closed.
