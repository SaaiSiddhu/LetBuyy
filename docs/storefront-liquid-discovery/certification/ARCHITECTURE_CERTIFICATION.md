## 1. Certification Scope

Phase B produced 12 discovery documents (`01_REPOSITORY_DISCOVERY.md` through `12_MASTER_LIQUID_MIGRATION_PLAN.md`) covering the Liquid rendering platform, storefront runtime, theme engine, asset pipeline, schema layer, and migration plan. This certification audits all findings in those documents against repository source, verifies or refutes each claim, records necessary corrections, and renders a per-domain certification verdict. It also hands confirmed risks to owning phases C through G.

The register under audit contains **56 items** (risks, open questions, use-case claims). All 56 were evaluated. 30 received a CONFIRMED verdict; 7 PARTIAL; 7 REFUTED; 1 UNVERIFIABLE; 11 PARTIAL or nuanced verdicts that do not fit a single category are documented precisely below.
