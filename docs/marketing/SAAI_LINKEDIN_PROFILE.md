I'm building LetBuyy — India's merchant-first commerce OS.

The problem I couldn't stop thinking about:

Indian merchants are running real businesses through WhatsApp DMs, manual COD tracking in notebooks, and Instagram messages. When they move to a platform, they get tools designed for a San Francisco merchant — USD-first pricing, Stripe checkout, no UPI, no Hindi, and a 2% transaction fee on every rupee they earn.

That's not a platform for Indian merchants. That's a Western product with a ₹ symbol.

LetBuyy is what commerce infrastructure looks like when you build from India's reality first:

→ 0% transaction fees
→ WhatsApp commerce native — catalog, cart, payment, tracking
→ UPI + COD first checkout (because 70% of India is still COD)
→ Hindi and regional language support
→ Built for Tier 2/3 India, not just metros

I started building this in August 2025, in my final year of B.Tech Artificial Intelligence at Dhanalakshmi Srinivasan College of Engineering, Coimbatore.

Before LetBuyy, I founded PixlNova — a product design and development agency. That work taught me what Indian businesses actually need from software: not the most complex stack, but the most reliable one.

I placed 3rd at an IBM Hackathon. Not impressive by global standards — but it was the first time I proved to myself I could build something no one else had built yet.

I'm 22. I'm from Salem, Tamil Nadu. I'm building for the merchant in the same Tier 2 India I grew up in.

I build in public. I share what I'm shipping, what's breaking, and what I'm learning. No polish. No PR.

If you're an Indian merchant, a D2C founder, an investor, or someone building for Bharat — let's connect.

## 11. WEEKLY POSTING GUIDE (After Profile Is Live)

Post 4–5 times per week. Rotate through these formats:

| Day | Format | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Observation | "Something I noticed about how Indian merchants handle returns..." |
| Tuesday | Build log | "What I shipped on LetBuyy this week — [feature] + what broke" |
| Wednesday | Question | "What's the biggest problem with COD order management? Genuinely asking." |
| Thursday | Lesson | "What talking to 10 merchants this month taught me" |
| Friday | Progress | "Week X of building LetBuyy. Numbers, lessons, what's next." |

**Rule:** Never post about LetBuyy features without connecting them to a merchant problem first.

**Rule:** One post = one idea. Don't cram 5 points into one post.

**Rule:** Short paragraphs. One sentence per line (LinkedIn formatting). White space is free.
