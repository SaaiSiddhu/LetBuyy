# LETBUYY 365-DAY CONTENT ROADMAP
**Document Type:** Annual Content + Campaign Calendar
**Version:** 1.0
**Year:** July 2026 – June 2027
**Milestone Target:** 0 → 10,000 merchants

---

## STRATEGIC FRAMEWORK

This roadmap is built around three overlapping realities:

1. **Product Maturity Gates:** Marketing amplifies what's ready. Content must be sequenced with product stability.
2. **Indian Commerce Calendar:** The festive season, harvest season, and fiscal calendar drive merchant behavior in ways that must be reflected in content.
3. **Trust Accumulation Model:** Each month builds on the last. Month 1 content is not abandoned in Month 6 — it forms the foundation that later content layers on top of.

**North Star Metric:** Merchant activations (stores that have processed at least 1 real order).

---

## PRE-LAUNCH PREREQUISITES (Before Month 1 Content Begins)

The following must be complete before the public content engine starts. This is not negotiable:

```
□ Fix unauthenticated account takeover vulnerability
□ Get merchant mobile login working on production
□ Publish pricing page (clear, honest, total cost visible)
□ Publish status/uptime page
□ Onboard 10 merchants manually with white-glove support
□ Get at least 1 merchant success story with real numbers (even small: ₹5,000 revenue)
□ Publish "Why I built LetBuyy" founder piece
□ Record "What is LetBuyy" 10-minute product walkthrough video
□ Create LetBuyy company LinkedIn page + founder LinkedIn profile
□ Create LetBuyy YouTube channel
□ Create LetBuyy Instagram account
□ Create Facebook Group "Indian D2C Sellers Network"
□ Verify letbuyy.com (primary domain fully working, letbuyy.store redirecting)
```

Only after this list is complete does the content calendar below apply.

**Monthly Theme:** *"Why LetBuyy Exists"*

5 consecutive days, 5 posts across all platforms about 5 real problems Indian merchants face. Not selling LetBuyy — documenting the problem LetBuyy exists to solve.

- Day 2: WhatsApp order chaos (YouTube Short + LinkedIn)
- Day 3: Hidden fees from platforms (X thread + LinkedIn)
- Day 4: Platform doesn't speak Hindi (Instagram Reel)
- Day 5: "Why I built LetBuyy" (founder YouTube video + LinkedIn article)

**Priority Content This Month:**
1. Founder's "Why I built LetBuyy" YouTube video (long-form, 15–20 min)
2. "What is LetBuyy" product overview video
3. Founder LinkedIn article: "The Indian Merchant Problem No One Is Solving"

### MONTH 2: PROVE IT WORKS

**Monthly Theme:** *"The Platform Is Real"*

**Key Campaign:** **"Live Setup Challenge"**
Record a live stream (or edited video) of setting up a complete LetBuyy store for a real merchant in real time. No cuts. No polish. Whatever happens, happens. Publish in full. This radical transparency builds more trust than a scripted demo.

**Priority Content This Month:**
1. "LetBuyy Live Setup" YouTube video (unedited, with a real merchant)
2. First merchant case study: Merchant #1's story with actual numbers
3. "LetBuyy vs Shopify India 2026" comparison video (YouTube SEO priority)
4. Instagram Carousels 1–5 (product education)
5. X: Start weekly build-in-public thread

**Campaign:** **"First 50 Merchants Founding Program"**
- Publicly announce: "We're looking for our first 50 founding merchants"
- Benefits: Lifetime discount / extended free trial / direct product input / founding merchant badge
- Application via simple form — screen for merchants with real businesses
- Publish the list of founding merchants (their names + store names) — social proof

**KPIs:**
- YouTube: 500 subscribers
- Merchants: 30 active
- Facebook Group: 300 members

2. Facebook Group: First community AMA with founder

**KPIs:**
- YouTube: 1,000 subscribers
- LinkedIn: 2,000 connections
- Merchants: 50 active
- Facebook Group: 500 members
