# LETBUYY SOCIAL MEDIA MASTER PLAN
**Document Type:** Platform Strategy
**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** June 2026

---

## STRATEGIC PREMISE

LetBuyy does not need to be everywhere. It needs to be deeply present where the decision to switch platforms is made. That decision is made in three places:

1. **LinkedIn** — where serious merchant-entrepreneurs evaluate tools
2. **YouTube** — where merchants research "how to" before committing
3. **WhatsApp** — where Indian merchants share recommendations peer-to-peer

Instagram and X are amplifiers and community-builders, not primary acquisition channels. Facebook is a merchant education channel. Prioritize accordingly.

**Phase 1 priority:** LinkedIn + YouTube + WhatsApp
**Phase 2 addition:** Instagram + X
**Phase 3 addition:** Facebook Groups

---

## PLATFORM 1: LINKEDIN

### Purpose
LinkedIn is where LetBuyy builds credibility with the decision-maker: the merchant who is serious enough to evaluate platforms, not just try free ones. It is also where:
- Investors discover LetBuyy
- Potential employees discover LetBuyy
- Press and analysts monitor LetBuyy
- D2C brand founders and growth marketers (who advise merchants) follow LetBuyy

LinkedIn is not a merchant acquisition channel for the bottom of the funnel — it is a trust signal that makes everything else work better.

### Audience
**Primary:** D2C founders, e-commerce operators, SMB merchants, India-focused startup ecosystem
**Secondary:** Investors, developers, logistics/fintech ecosystem, potential hires
**Tertiary:** Media, analysts, competitor employees (useful for intelligence)

### The Founder Account vs. Company Page

This is the most important strategic decision on LinkedIn.

**Founder account gets 10–50× more organic reach than a company page.** The founder IS the brand at this stage. The company page matters for ads, hiring, and brand legitimacy — but the founder's feed is the real distribution engine.

**Founder account strategy:**
- Post 3–5× per week
- Mix: 40% build-in-public, 30% startup lessons, 20% India commerce insights, 10% personal story
- Engage in comments on posts by D2C founders, commerce operators, merchant community
- DM personally with merchants who comment or react to build 1:1 relationships
- Cross-post or reference company page for major announcements

**Company page strategy:**
- Post 1× per day (can repost from founder or team with added context)
- Content: product updates, merchant stories, job postings, event announcements
- Maintain professional tone — company page is the "official record"
- Grow followers with ads targeting: India + D2C founder + SMB owner + e-commerce titles

### Content Types for LinkedIn

**1. Build-in-Public Posts (highest engagement)**
Format: "What we shipped this week / What we learned / What's next"
Frequency: Weekly (Friday)
Example: "We rebuilt our checkout flow this week. Here's the before/after and why it matters for a merchant in Tier 2 India who has 60% COD orders. Thread 🧵"

**2. Merchant Case Studies**
Format: Real numbers, real merchant, real journey
Frequency: 2× per month (as merchants onboard)
Example: "Priya sells handmade jewelry from Jaipur. She was on Instagram DMs and manually tracking 40 orders/day in a notebook. 30 days on LetBuyy: ₹1.4L revenue, 0 missed orders, automated COD notifications."

**3. India Commerce Insights**
Format: Data-backed observations about Indian e-commerce
Frequency: Weekly
Example: "The Indian merchant using a smartphone manages 3 platforms, 2 payment gateways, and a logistics aggregator every day. The software stack isn't fragmented by choice — no platform solved all three. We're trying to."

**4. Engineering Deep Dives** (Founder + technical co-founder)
Format: Architecture decisions, scaling challenges, honest mistakes
Frequency: 2× per month
Example: "We chose to build identity as a separate service before we had any merchants. Here's why that was both correct and premature — and what we'd do differently."

**5. Startup Journey Posts**
Format: Raw, honest moments from building LetBuyy
Frequency: Weekly
Example: "We had 3 merchants this month. Here are the exact conversations that changed our roadmap."

**6. Industry Takes / Opinion**
Format: Strong opinion about India e-commerce or platform economics
Frequency: Weekly
Example: "The 2% transaction fee isn't a fee. It's a rent. And most merchant platforms are landlords pretending to be partners."

**7. Hiring Posts**
Format: Real role descriptions + why LetBuyy is a compelling place to work
Frequency: As needed (when hiring)
Design these to double as brand content — what LetBuyy believes in + what they need

### Posting Frequency

| Content Type | Frequency | Account |
|---|---|---|
| Build-in-public | Weekly (Friday) | Founder |
| India commerce insight | Weekly (Tuesday) | Founder |
| Startup journey | Weekly (Wednesday) | Founder |
| Industry take | Weekly (Monday) | Founder |
| Merchant case study | 2×/month | Company page + Founder |
| Engineering deep dive | 2×/month | Founder/CTO |
| Product update | With each release | Company page |
| Hiring post | As needed | Company page + Founder |
| Repost/comment engagement | Daily | Founder |

### Growth Strategy

**Month 1-3:** Build the founder's network from 0. Connect daily with: D2C founders, e-commerce operators, logistics founders, SMB tech builders, investors in India consumer tech. Target: 500 relevant connections.

**Month 3-6:** Start appearing in targeted comment sections. Add genuine value to posts by D2C founders with large followings. Don't sell. Contribute. This creates inbound curiosity.

**Month 6-12:** Guest posts in major India commerce/startup publications (Your Story, Inc42, Economic Times Startup). Cross-promote on LinkedIn. Each press piece = a week of LinkedIn content.

**Partnership Signal Strategy:** Tag strategic partners in relevant posts (shipping partners, payment partners). This gives reach + credibility + signals ecosystem.

## PLATFORM 2: YOUTUBE

### Purpose

YouTube is LetBuyy's highest-leverage trust channel. A merchant who watches a 10-minute LetBuyy tutorial before signing up is infinitely more likely to activate than one who saw a banner ad. YouTube is where:

1. Merchants learn how LetBuyy works before committing
2. Merchants learn how to run their business (builds authority beyond the product)
3. Search engines surface LetBuyy content to merchants searching for help
4. Investors/press do due diligence

YouTube has the highest ROI of any content channel for LetBuyy — a video made today will generate trust and signups for 5 years.

### Audience

**Primary:** First-time online sellers, established Instagram merchants considering a platform, Meesho/Flipkart sellers wanting to build their own brand, D2C founders wanting a Shopify alternative

**Secondary:** E-commerce developers, logistics operators, payment gateway evaluators

**Language priority:** Hindi videos first, then English. Hindi unlocks 400M+ YouTube users. English positions for premium merchant segment.

### Content Categories

**1. "LetBuyy in 10 Minutes" (Core product demo)**
Show the entire platform in one video. This is your most important video. Reshoot it every time the product meaningfully improves.

**2. "Setup Series" (Tutorial playlist)**
Step-by-step setup: Domain → Store design → Products → Payments → Shipping → First sale. Each video is standalone AND part of a series.

**3. "Merchant Education" (Builds authority)**
How to manage COD returns. How to set up GST billing. How to write product descriptions that rank on Google. How to run WhatsApp marketing campaigns. These are not about LetBuyy — they build trust by being useful.

**4. "Build-in-Public" (Startup transparency)**
Monthly video: "What we shipped, what we broke, what we learned." Raw and honest. This is not a PR exercise. This is the antidote to polished startup theater.

**5. "Merchant Stories"**
15–20 minute documentary-style: A real merchant, their journey, their numbers. Film them at their business. Show the real operations. Scripted testimonials are worthless. Real stories are invaluable.

**6. "India Commerce Insights"**
"Why India's festive season is different from any market in the world." "The economics of COD — why it's 70% and what it costs you." Deep dives on commerce mechanics that make LetBuyy seem like an authority, not just a vendor.

**7. "Engineering Stories"**
"Why we rebuilt our identity platform." "How we handle 1000 orders/second during Diwali sales." For developers evaluating LetBuyy and for building brand credibility with technical audiences.

### Shorts Strategy

Every long-form video is a source of 5–10 Shorts. Extract:
- The most surprising fact
- The key tip
- The product "wow moment"
- The before/after comparison

Shorts drive channel discovery. Long-form drives conversion. Run both.

Dedicated Shorts series ideas:
- "60-second store setup tips"
- "Did you know LetBuyy can..." (feature reveal)
- "Merchant Stat of the Week"
- "WhatsApp Commerce Quick Tips"

### SEO Strategy

Every video title should contain what a merchant Googles, not what LetBuyy wants to say:

| Wrong Title | Right Title |
|---|---|
| "Introducing LetBuyy Checkout" | "How to Set Up COD Orders on LetBuyy (Step by Step)" |
| "LetBuyy WhatsApp Commerce" | "Send Your Customers Order Updates on WhatsApp - LetBuyy Tutorial" |
| "Our New Dashboard" | "How to Track Your Daily Sales on LetBuyy" |
| "LetBuyy vs Shopify" | "Shopify vs LetBuyy India 2026 - Which Is Better for Indian Sellers?" |

Chapters in every video (for YouTube SEO and watch time).
Pinned comment with key links, resources, and call to action.
Custom thumbnails: face + text + problem statement (e.g., "I lost ₹40,000 in orders until I did this").

### Posting Cadence

| Period | Cadence |
|---|---|
| Months 1-3 | 1 long-form/week + 3 Shorts/week |
| Months 4-6 | 2 long-form/week + daily Shorts |
| Months 7-12 | 3 long-form/week + daily Shorts |

### Founder Channel Strategy

Consider a separate founder YouTube channel (or vlog playlist) for:
- Personal startup journey
- India commerce commentary
- Behind-the-scenes of building LetBuyy

This is distinct from the product channel and creates human connection that a product channel can't.

## PLATFORM 3: INSTAGRAM

### Purpose

Instagram is where LetBuyy reaches the largest pool of potential merchants: the direct-to-consumer seller who currently sells via DMs, stories, and payment links. This person is already on Instagram. They already have a following. They are the merchant LetBuyy most needs.

Instagram is also where LetBuyy's visual identity lives. Every piece of Instagram content should look like a brand a merchant would want to build on.

### Audience

**Primary:** Instagram-native sellers (fashion, beauty, food, art, home decor), small brands selling via DMs, aspiring D2C founders

**Secondary:** D2C customers who might refer their sellers to LetBuyy, startup community

### Content Mix

| Format | % of Content | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Reels | 50% | Discovery + virality |
| Carousels | 30% | Education + saves |
| Stories | 15% | Community + real-time |
| Static posts | 5% | Announcements |

### Reels Strategy

Reels are the discovery engine. Every Reel should be watchable by someone who has never heard of LetBuyy.

**Hook formula:** First 3 seconds must contain the merchant's problem, not LetBuyy's solution.

- "Managing 50 COD orders a day in WhatsApp messages? Here's what that costs you."
- "Your Instagram shop has no checkout. Here's why that's losing you 40% of sales."
- "The real cost of using Shopify in India (they hide this in the fine print)."

**Reel categories:**
1. Product demos (show, don't tell)
2. Merchant problem/solution
3. India commerce education
4. Behind-the-scenes of LetBuyy building
5. Merchant spotlights (their story in 60 seconds)
6. "Did you know" commerce tips

### Carousel Strategy

Carousels are the education engine. They get saved. They get shared. They rank in Instagram search.

**Best performing carousel topics:**
1. "10 things your platform should do for free in India"
2. "The real cost of COD returns (and how to reduce them)"
3. "How to set up your WhatsApp business catalog in 5 steps"
4. "Before and after: switching from Instagram DMs to a real store"
5. "GST for online sellers: what you need to know"

### Stories Strategy

Stories are for community and real-time engagement:
- Polls: "Are you using WhatsApp to take orders?" (data collection + engagement)
- Question boxes: "What's your biggest shipping headache?"
- Behind-the-scenes: real product/engineering updates
- Merchant shoutouts
- Limited-time offers/beta access

### Highlights Structure

Maintain these permanent Highlights:
1. **What is LetBuyy** — 3–5 stories explaining the platform
2. **Merchant Stories** — real merchant spotlights
3. **Features** — one highlight per major feature
4. **Tutorials** — link to YouTube tutorials
5. **WhatsApp Commerce** — dedicated feature showcase
6. **Pricing** — clear, honest pricing breakdown

## PLATFORM 4: X (TWITTER)

### Purpose

X has two distinct audiences for LetBuyy that are both valuable but require different content:

1. **India startup/tech community** — founders, developers, investors, journalists. This is where LetBuyy gets seen by people who amplify it to their audiences.

2. **Build-in-public community** — the global and India-specific audience that follows founders building companies in public. LetBuyy should be one of the most transparent startups in India on X.

X is not a merchant acquisition channel in the way Instagram or YouTube are. It is a credibility amplifier and ecosystem builder.

### Content Strategy

**Thread format (highest engagement):**
- "Thread: Why we chose [X] architecture decision and what we got wrong"
- "Thread: The real economics of India COD — a breakdown for founders"
- "Thread: What 3 months of talking to Indian merchants taught us"

**Quick takes (daily):**
- Observations about India commerce
- Product milestone updates ("We just shipped X")
- Honest reflections on startup building

**Technical content (2×/week):**
- Architecture decisions
- Engineering challenges
- Tech stack choices and why

**Engagement strategy:**
- Participate in India startup conversations
- Engage with D2C founder posts
- Engage with India tech investor posts
- Share merchant wins publicly (with permission)

### Build-in-Public on X

The build-in-public community on X is one of the strongest trust-building mechanisms available to a pre-revenue startup. Follow the Pieter Levels / Levels.io model: radical transparency about numbers, progress, and decisions.

Weekly "build log":
- Lines of code shipped
- Features launched
- Merchants onboarded
- Revenue (when it exists)
- Biggest problem this week
- What we're shipping next week

## PLATFORM 5: FACEBOOK

### Purpose

Facebook is LetBuyy's mass merchant acquisition channel — but not via the newsfeed. The strategic play is Facebook Groups.

Indian merchants have millions of active participants in Facebook Groups:
- "D2C India Founders"
- "Online Selling Tips India"
- "Resellers Network India"
- "E-Commerce India"
- "Home Business India"

These groups are where merchants share recommendations, ask questions, and make platform decisions collectively.

**Strategy:** Don't advertise into these groups. Become an indispensable contributor. Answer questions about COD management, WhatsApp commerce, GST invoicing, platform comparisons. Become known as the people who know e-commerce in India — not just as "the LetBuyy people."

### LetBuyy Facebook Group

Create: **"Indian D2C Sellers Community"** (not "LetBuyy Users")

The group name should be about the community, not the company. Welcome competitors' users. Welcome people who've never heard of LetBuyy. Become the best place on Facebook for Indian online sellers to learn.

Content mix for the group:
- Weekly AMA with the LetBuyy founder
- Merchant spotlight: members share their wins
- Weekly commerce tip (not about LetBuyy specifically)
- Platform Q&A (honest — including when competitors are better for a specific use case)
- Job board for merchant teams
- Vendor recommendations (shipping, packaging, photography, etc.)

The group works because it creates genuine value independent of LetBuyy. Users who trust the community will trust LetBuyy.

## CROSS-PLATFORM CONTENT SYSTEM

Every piece of content should work across multiple platforms with adaptation, not recreation:

```
YouTube long-form video
    ↓
Shorts (3–5 from each long video)
    ↓
Instagram Reels (repurposed Short with caption)
    ↓
LinkedIn post (key insight + link to video)
    ↓
X thread (breakdown of key points)
    ↓
WhatsApp message/broadcast (summary + link)
    ↓
Facebook Group post (discussion starter)
```

One long-form video = 15–20 pieces of distributed content. This is the content multiplication system that makes a small team capable of being everywhere.

## PAID STRATEGY

**Do not run paid ads until organic trust signals exist.**

Running ads to a product with no social proof, no reviews, and no merchant stories is burning money. Paid works when organic content already proves the product works.

**Phase 1 (Months 1-6):** Zero paid. Build organic signals only.

**Phase 2 (Months 7-12):** Retargeting only. Retarget people who watched YouTube tutorials or visited the pricing page. These are warm leads who have already begun trust-building.

**Phase 3 (Year 2):** Prospecting ads to lookalike audiences based on your first 1,000 merchants.

**Ad creative principle:** The best ad is content that doesn't look like an ad. A merchant case study. A comparison video. A tutorial with a CTA. Not a banner.
