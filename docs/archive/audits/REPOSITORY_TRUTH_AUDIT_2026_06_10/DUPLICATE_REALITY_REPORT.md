## 1. Executive Summary

1. **The defining duplication is temporal, not spatial.** LetBuyy is three coexisting platforms in one repository: `main` (the only branch production CI deploys from — `production-deploy.yml:3-7` `on: push: branches: [main]`), `stepup/main` HEAD (+30 commits), and the uncommitted working tree (Phases A–D13). For governance, identity, money, themes, validators, deploy matrices, and mobile, these are **three different systems with three different security postures** (§2). The canonical reality (worktree) is the *only* one that conforms to the canon — and it is the only one with zero version-control protection.
2. **Every load-bearing domain still has at least two live implementations.** The forensic master's "two parallel systems" anti-pattern (`ANTI-R1`, `AGENTS.md:314`) reproduces in this audit across **10 cluster families and ~60 documented duplicate pairs**: two governance regimes, ≥4 auth planes + 2 Turnstile verifiers, 2 money models with 3 refund paths and 3+1 `merchant_payable` writers, 2 theme renderers + 2 schema sources + 2 compose paths, 2 commerce backends (gateway + ~62 merchant-control-plane API routes), 4 tenancy access planes, 6 validator aliases for 1 validator + 6 duplicated assertions, duplicated prompt families, 2 task/memory systems, and a mobile app with 2 navigation systems and same-name duplicate widget classes.
3. **The canon already names a winner for almost every pair.** `LETBUYY_CANONICAL_SYSTEMS.md` Rule C1 ("one system per domain, no coexistence") plus the §2 master registry resolves ~90% of the pairs found here; root `AGENTS.md` `WIN-R3` resolves the four flagship ones (ledger > legacy capture, Liquid > React, identity-platform > gateway auth, RLS+GUC > app-guard). The duplicates in this report are therefore not undecided conflicts — they are **decided conflicts whose losers are still alive**, mostly because (a) the deciding documents are uncommitted, (b) the winner is undeployed/unproven, or (c) a validator structurally mandates the loser's existence (§4.1.3, §9.7.5).
4. **Two duplications are self-inflicted by the new law itself.** (i) The end-state validator and survivability validator **require** the rejected multi-region/chaos artifacts to exist (`whole-repo-end-state-validator.mjs:172-193`; `survivability-governance-validator.mjs:10-30,93-108`) while the Constitution forbids certifying them (`LETBUYY_CONSTITUTION.md:98,112`) — law mandating its own loser. (ii) The promoted root `AGENTS.md` and its `GOVERNANCE_REWRITE` source copy now disagree on CODE-R1 (3-hunk divergence) — **two texts of DOC 02**.
5. **The most dangerous duplicate pair for users is auth:** committed reality (`main`/HEAD) mints gateway `randomHex(32)` sessions and carries the `/api/auth/switch` account-takeover; only the worktree has the asymmetric identity-issued topology and the fix. The most dangerous for money: the storefront capture path that every buyer hits posts **zero** canonical ledger entries in *all three* realities (`confirm_order_payment_with_ledger`, `000_baseline.sql:1590`) — the one major pair where even the worktree has not yet made the winner live.
