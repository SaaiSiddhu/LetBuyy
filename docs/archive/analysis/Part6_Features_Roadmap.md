## 4. Revenue Model (Detailed)

### Subscription Revenue
```
Projection (10,000 users):
  Free: 85% = 8,500 users × $0 = $0/mo
  Starter: 10% = 1,000 × $9 = $9,000/mo
  Business: 4% = 400 × $29 = $11,600/mo
  Enterprise: 1% = 100 × $79 = $7,900/mo
  Subscription MRR: $28,500/mo ($342,000 ARR)
```

### Platform Fees & Commissions
```
Transaction fees: avg 0.8% × est. $600K GMV/mo = ~$5,000/mo
Theme marketplace: 30% commission, est. ~$2,000/mo
App Store: 20% commission, est. ~$1,000/mo
Total MRR: ~$36,500/mo ($438K ARR)
```

### Marketplace Revenue Split
- Theme developers: 70% dev / 30% platform
- App developers: 80% dev / 20% platform
- Monthly payouts, NET 30, minimum $50
