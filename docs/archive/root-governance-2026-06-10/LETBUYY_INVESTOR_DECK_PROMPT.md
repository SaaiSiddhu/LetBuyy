## INSTRUCTIONS TO THE DECK GENERATOR

You are not a slide decorator. Build this as if you were a partner at Sequoia / Peak XV / Accel / a16z / Y Combinator who has also shipped product at Shopify and Stripe. The reader is a seed investor who sees 50 decks a week. Your job is not to list features — it is to **manufacture belief** that LetBuyy can become the Commerce Operating System for India and, later, the emerging world.

Rules:

- **One idea per slide.** A partner should grasp each slide in under 5 seconds. Headline carries the argument; body is support, not a paragraph.
- **Show, don't tell.** Every slide needs an intentional visual: a diagram, a chart, an architecture map, a flywheel, or a bold typographic statement. No clip-art, no stock photos, no generic icons-in-a-row.
- **Tension then resolution.** The first third builds an undeniable problem. The middle resolves it with LetBuyy. The end makes the size of the prize feel inevitable.
- **Seed-appropriate honesty.** This is a bet on insight, wedge, and team — not on big traction. Lead with the "why now" and the wedge. Where traction is thin, replace it with sharp design partners, a credible roadmap, and proof of build velocity.
- **45 slides** including appendix. Main narrative ≈ 35 slides; the rest are appendix slides investors reference in diligence.
- Produce, alongside the slides: **speaker notes** per slide (already drafted below — refine, don't replace) and keep the **appendix** as real slides at the end.

Tone: premium, technical, founder-led, future-facing. Think Stripe x Linear x Shopify Investor Day. Confident, never hypey. Specific, never vague.

## BRAND & DESIGN SYSTEM

**Color tokens (use exactly):**

- Brand Purple `#8B5CF6` — primary brand, key accents, the LetBuyy "signature."
- Deep Blue `#4462FF` — secondary action / data emphasis.
- Periwinkle `#8EA2FF` — soft highlights, secondary lines, gradients.
- Cyan `#06B6D4` — energy / "live" data, positive deltas, callouts.
- Deep Navy `#0A0F1A` — primary dark background.
- Dark Navy `#0F1729` — panel / card background on dark.
- Slate `#94A3B8` — secondary text, labels, axis lines.
- White `#FFFFFF` — primary text on dark, clean light-mode background.

**Usage:**

- Default to **dark mode** (Deep Navy `#0A0F1A` background, White text) for vision/story slides; use **light mode** (White background, Deep Navy text) for dense data, architecture, and competitive slides so they read clearly.
- One gradient only: Brand Purple → Deep Blue → Cyan, used sparingly for hero moments and flywheels. Never rainbow.
- Accent discipline: purple is the brand, cyan is "live/now," everything else is structure.

**Typography:** A single modern grotesque/geometric sans (e.g., Inter, General Sans, or Söhne-like). Huge headline weights (600–700), generous tracking on labels (uppercase, letter-spaced Slate), tons of whitespace. Numbers are a design element — set big.

**Layout language:** 12-column grid, wide margins, left-aligned headlines, one focal visual per slide. Thin 1px lines in Slate/Periwinkle for structure. Subtle dot-grid or topographic line texture allowed on dark hero slides only. Consistent footer: `LetBuyy · Commerce OS for India · Confidential` + slide number.

**Forbidden:** Canva-template feel, stocky 3D blobs, drop-shadow heavy cards, emoji, more than 2 type sizes per slide, walls of bullet text.

## Appendix A — Hard Questions, Straight Answers

> Put these on slides as a clean Q→A list (or hold as speaker prep and pull a single Q forward as a full slide if an investor pushes). Each answer is one breath.

- **Why not Shopify?** It's the best in the world — for cards, web, prepaid, metro. In India it has no Shopify Payments, so merchants pay ~4–5% all-in per order and get a card/web-first checkout. India is an edge case in its architecture; it's the center of ours.
- **Why not Dukaan / Instamojo?** Great for a fast, cheap launch — but they're tools, not an operating system. They hit a ceiling on customization, payments depth, settlements, and the unified ledger. We start where they top out.
- **Why not WooCommerce?** It's free until you count the plugins, hosting, and the sysadmin you become. Self-hosted complexity is the opposite of what a Tier-2 merchant needs. We're managed, India-native, and coherent.
- **Why not just Razorpay + Shiprocket + a WhatsApp tool?** That's the status quo — and it's the problem. Five world-class rails with no OS on top still leaves the merchant as the systems integrator, with five take-rates and no shared ledger. We re-bundle them merchant-side.
- **Why not Meesho?** Meesho is a marketplace: it rents you demand and owns the customer and the brand. We give the merchant their own storefront, customer, and operating system. Different game.
- **Why now?** UPI at ₹30T/month, WhatsApp at 535M users as the storefront, Tier-2/3 as the majority of new orders, and the rails freshly unbundled. Five years ago the primitives weren't all there. Now they are — and no one has re-bundled them.
- **What's defensible?** The ledger (re-platforming cost), the commerce graph (compounding proprietary data), the ecosystem (apps/themes/partners), and India regulatory/PA depth. Moats that accrue with scale, not features that can be copied.
- **How do you scale (GTM)?** A shareable UPI-checkout wedge that spreads merchant-to-merchant, Tier-2/3 community and reseller distribution, partner channels, and a developer/agency long tail — low CAC by construction, then expansion revenue inside each account.
- **Why India first?** Because India's primitives are uniquely favorable (UPI, WhatsApp, GST digitization, Tier-2 demand) and uniquely underserved by global platforms. Win here and you've built the playbook for every emerging market.
- **Why can this become massive?** Software + services layer on a $25–30B MSME opportunity inside a $330B market, with >100% NRR economics and an export path to every market that shares India's pattern. The OS layer is winner-take-most.

## Appendix B — Risks & Mitigations

> One slide, two columns: Risk → How we de-risk. Be candid; investors trust founders who name their own risks.

- **Razorpay / Shiprocket move up the stack into the OS.** Mitigation: they're structurally incentivized to stay neutral rails serving all platforms; moving up risks their own distribution. We start ledger-first and merchant-side, which they'd have to rebuild. Speed and focus are our edge.
- **Shopify localizes for India (adds UPI Payments, COD, WhatsApp).** Mitigation: their global architecture and pricing model resist India-native rebuilds; they monetize the payment flow we deliberately don't. We compete on alignment and depth, not on catching up to features.
