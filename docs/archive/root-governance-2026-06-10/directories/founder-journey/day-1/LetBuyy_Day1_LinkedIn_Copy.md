## 1. Final LinkedIn Post — English

Most people my age are trying to finish college.
I'm trying to build something that might outlive it.

I'm Saai — a student. And most days, once classes are done, I'm building LetBuyy.

Let me be honest about where things stand: there's no launch yet. No customers. No revenue. No "overnight success" story to hand you. What I have instead is a pile of unfinished work and a strange amount of patience for it.

The last few weeks haven't looked impressive. I've been tearing down things I built wrong the first time and redesigning foundations nobody will ever see — the boring parts that quietly decide whether the exciting parts ever get to exist.

It would be faster to fake it. Put up a slick landing page, announce a launch, collect the applause. But I don't want something that looks ready. I want something that actually is.

Here's what this is teaching me: most things worth building are slow. College takes four years. The things I admire most took ten. Rushing is just borrowing speed from the future and paying it back with interest.

So I'm going to document the slow version — the real one. The late nights, the rebuilds, the small wins, and the mistakes I'll almost certainly make again.

This is Day 1.

If you've ever built something quietly, long before it was ready — follow along. I'd really love to have you here for the long version.

— Saai, building LetBuyy

Idhu enakku kaththukudukkuradhu: worth-aana ellaame slow-a thaan build aagum. College-ku naalu varusham. Naan romba admire pannra ellaame paththu varusham eduthuchu. Rush panradhu-nnaa — future kitta irundhu speed-a kadan vaangi, interest-oda thirumba kattradhu.

So naan slow version-a document panren — real-aanadha. Late nights, rebuilds, small wins, and naan kandippa thirumba pannapora mistakes.

Idhu thaan Day 1.
