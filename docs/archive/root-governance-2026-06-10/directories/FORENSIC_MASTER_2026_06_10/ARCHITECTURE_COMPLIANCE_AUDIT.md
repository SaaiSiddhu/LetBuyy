## 0. Methodology

1. V1 was read in full and decomposed into testable contract points: 12 principles (§2), every approved component (§3), 15 rejections (§4), the deferral table (§5), topology (§6), DB architecture (§7), payments (§8), checkout (§9), merchant OS (§10), WhatsApp (§11), and Appendix B's 15 forced conflicts.
2. Every claim was verified against the **current working tree only** (which contains uncommitted changes — audited as-is, `git status` shows 20 modified files including the identity-platform auth fixes). Prior audits (`FOUNDATION_FORENSICS_2026_06_09/`, `PHASE2_ARB_CHALLENGE_2026_06_09/`, `LETBUYY_FOUNDATION_AUTHORITY/`, `FOUNDATION_AUTOPSY/`, `FOUNDATION_STEPUP_LEDGER.md` wave claims) were treated as **hypothesis lists only**; every conclusion below was re-derived from source. Items those audits flagged that are now fixed are explicitly marked **FIXED**.
3. Every verdict carries `file:line` evidence. Where a claim could not be proven either way it is marked **UNVERIFIED**.
4. Verdict vocabulary (exactly one per row): **APPROVED** (exists and complies) · **PARTIAL** (exists, incomplete/divergent) · **VIOLATION** (contradicts V1) · **DUPLICATE** (capability implemented 2+ places) · **DEPRECATED** (V1 deprecated it, still present/wired) · **DEAD** (claimed/scaffolded, no real wiring) · **MISSING** (V1 requires it, absent).
5. No code was modified. This document is the only artifact produced.

## 1. Executive Compliance Verdict

**Overall compliance: ≈62% — "the skeleton obeys the freeze; the bloodstream and the law books do not."**

What V1 ordered as *topology* has substantially happened: there is exactly one commerce monolith (`services/api-gateway`), the six reserved services are genuinely manifest-only, no deprecated app alias has been recreated, migrations resumed at 138 with rollbacks for the V1-scope set, the canonical double-entry ledger with the eight named accounts exists with append-only and balance triggers, the payment-forgery fix and the `provider_payment_id` unique index are live in the working tree, the server-authoritative cart with a signed `checkout_url` and an idempotent checkout state machine exist, WhatsApp is correctly scoped to lifecycle + COD-confirm + consent, and the three historical S0s (payment forgery, `/api/auth/switch` account takeover, fabricated payout success) are **FIXED** in the current tree.

What V1 ordered as *business and governance* has not happened:

- **The GMV rake V1 ordered killed is not only alive — it grew.** Payout creation deducts a defaulted **300 bps (3%) platform commission plus 18% GST on the fee from every merchant payout** (`services/api-gateway/src/routes/payouts.ts:344,398-401`; auto-seeded at `routes/commission.ts:202`), while the marketing site simultaneously promises "LetBuyy does not charge any transaction fees" (`apps/marketing-site/src/components/FAQ.tsx:17-18`). This is a direct violation of Principle 9 and R-14.
- **The payout rail drifted from PA-split to self-custody-shaped.** V1 §8 mandates Razorpay Route / Cashfree split (no custody, R-6); the runtime hardcodes rail `razorpayx` (`routes/payouts.ts:119-120`), a from-own-account disbursement product.
- **The hosted checkout origin does not exist.** The gateway signs URLs to `https://checkout.<domain>/checkout` but no page, middleware, rewrite, or DNS record serves that host (no `/checkout` root route in `apps/storefront-runtime/src/app/`; `infrastructure/dns/dns.tf` still manages only `letbuyy.store` with no `checkout` record).
- **The two-constitution problem is structural, enforced, and unresolved.** `AGENTS.md` + `scripts/validators/governance-common.mjs:57-105` define nine canonical web apps (including the marketplace/console/portal apps V1 §6 omits and R-11 rejects) and the 196-validator gate actively *requires* those roots to exist and *requires* CQRS/event-store/replay/projection/saga/multi-region governance artifacts that V1 rejected (R-4, R-5, R-13). Nothing in CI enforces V1; the machine enforces the constitution V1 superseded.
- **Several load-bearing kernels are DDL-real but wiring-dead:** scoped compensation rows are written but no executor exists; the stale-reservation sweeper function has zero callers; the canonical-ledger reconciliation that the wallet endpoint *requires* before showing a balance has no scheduled invoker.

The honest characterization: **V1's P0 engineering is roughly two-thirds genuinely built — materially better than the 2026-06-09 forensics baseline — but the repository is still governed by the rejected constitution, still monetized by the rejected rake, and still missing the single buyer-facing surface (hosted checkout) the freeze called the point of the exercise.**
