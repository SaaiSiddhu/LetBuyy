## 0. The One Rule That Makes This Work

You are not marketing a product. You are **documenting a build**. The audience is not buying software — they are subscribing to a story where they want to know what happens next. Every post answers one of three questions: *Who is this person? · Can I trust them? · Do I want to see tomorrow?*

If a post doesn't build trust, curiosity, or credibility — kill it.

## 1. Founder Profile & Voice

**Who Saai is in public:**
- A college student who chose the hard thing: building a commerce platform for Indian merchants instead of coasting.
- Honest about the stage: foundations, not launch. Rebuilding identity, auth, design system, merchant OS, checkout.
- Grinding the unglamorous parts: late nights, part-time work, loans, gym, exams — all at once.
- Big vision (give small Indian merchants a real alternative), small current footprint (working toward *first* beta merchants).

**Voice principles:**
1. **Specific over vague.** Not "worked on the backend" → "spent 6 hours rebuilding how login sessions switch between accounts."
2. **Honest over hype.** Never inflate. "Long way to go" is a feature, not a bug — it's why people root for you.
3. **Teach as you learn.** You're 1 step ahead of your audience on most things. Share the step.
4. **Show the cost.** The struggle (money, time, sleep) is the emotional engine. Don't hide it; don't wallow in it.
5. **India-rooted.** UPI, WhatsApp, COD, small-town merchants, rupee math. This is your home advantage.

**Brand pillars (every post ladders to one):**

## 2. Anti-Fake Guardrails (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

Never create or imply: fake revenue, fake users, fake merchants, fake testimonials, fake screenshots of traction, fake launches, fake "we just hit X."

Allowed and encouraged: real code/architecture work, real struggles, real lessons, real small wins ("got auth switching to work in test"), real vision, real questions to the audience, *clearly-labeled* mockups/concepts.

**The honesty test before posting:** *If a beta merchant or an engineer read this, would they catch me exaggerating?* If yes, rewrite.

## 7. MONTH 2 — The Weekly Adaptive Content Engine

Month 2 is not pre-scripted post-by-post. Instead you run a **weekly generation ritual** that turns the real week into content. This keeps it authentic and removes the "what do I post" decision.

### The Sunday Ritual (45 min)
1. **Brain-dump the week** into `weekly-log.md` (template below).
2. **Pick the week's theme** from the Month‑2 arc (W5–W8, Section 4).
3. **Run the generation prompt** (below) to draft the week's posts.
4. **Edit for honesty + voice**, slot into the rhythm grid (Section 5).
5. **Build visuals** by duplicating the 5 Canva templates.

### `weekly-log.md` template
```
# Week of <date> — Theme: <W5–W8 theme>
## What I built / worked on
- (be specific: files, features, decisions)
## What broke / what was hard
-
## What I learned (technical, business, personal)
-
## Smallest real win
-
## A number I can honestly share (hours, commits, bugs fixed — NOT fake users)
-
## A question I'm sitting with
-
## Life this week (college, gym, money, energy)
-
```

### The weekly generation prompt (paste into Claude each Sunday)
```
You are my founder content engine. Here is my real weekly log: <paste weekly-log.md>.
This week's arc theme is: <theme>. Generate, in my voice (honest, specific, India-rooted,
no fake metrics, balanced Tanglish where the platform fits):
- 3 LinkedIn posts (English; optional 1-line Tanglish accent)
- 10 X posts (mix English/Tanglish) + 1 Sunday recap thread
- 2 IG reel scripts + 2 carousel outlines (English + Tanglish caption)
- 1 vlog concept
Map each to the weekly rhythm grid. Flag anything that risks exaggeration.
```

> Month‑2 platform files contain a **fully worked example week (W5)** so you see the output quality to aim for, plus reusable templates for W6–W8.

## 8. MONTH 3 — The Daily Founder Journal → Content Engine

By Month 3 you graduate to a **daily** system. You journal 5 minutes a day; the engine turns 7 journal entries into a full week of content. This is the long-term machine you keep running after Day 90.

### The daily 5-minute journal (`journal/YYYY-MM-DD.md`)
```
Date:
One line — what today was really about:
Built/decided:
Struggled with:
Learned:
Felt:
Quotable thought (one sentence I'd say out loud):
Shareable? (Y/N — is there a non-sensitive nugget here?)
```

### The daily micro-extraction (2 min, same day)
Each day, lift **one** quotable thought → schedule as the next morning's X post. That alone keeps X alive daily with zero extra writing.

### The weekly compile prompt (Sunday, Month 3)
```
Here are my 7 daily journals: <paste journal/*.md for the week>.
Arc theme: <W9–W12 theme>. Turn these into:
- 3 LinkedIn posts (one MUST be a "progress vs Day 1" reflection)
- the week of X (reuse the daily quotables + 1 recap thread)
- 2 IG reels + 2 carousels
- 1 vlog + (if W12) the 90-day review script
Keep it honest. Where I have a real number, use it. Where I don't, don't invent one.
Surface the single best "what happens next" hook to drive follows + waitlist.
```

### Why this order (Month 1 → 2 → 3)
- **Month 1 scripted:** removes friction while the habit forms.
- **Month 2 weekly:** you start converting real work into content; lower lift, higher authenticity.
- **Month 3 daily:** the self-sustaining engine — journaling becomes the only input, content becomes the output.

## 9. Audience Growth Strategy (per platform)

**Universal:** Consistency > virality. Reply to every comment for the first 60 days. Your first 1,000 followers come from *conversation*, not reach.

- **LinkedIn (credibility engine):** 3 posts/week, post 8–10am IST, first comment with extra context (boosts reach), engage 20 min before + after posting. Tag no one for clout. Comment thoughtfully on 5 bigger India-startup/founder posts daily. Goal: become "the college kid building real commerce infra."
- **X / Twitter (the build-log + network):** 1–2/day, build-in-public is the wedge. Reply to founders/devtools/India-fintech accounts. Use the Sunday recap thread as your weekly "story episode." Quote-tweet your own old posts to show progress over time.
- **Instagram (the human + reach):** Reels carry reach; carousels carry saves; stories carry intimacy. Face on camera. Hooks in first 1.5s. Tanglish lands hardest here. Goal: the *relatable* founder.
- **YouTube (the depth + trust):** 1 long-form/week + the weekly vlog. This is where skeptics go to decide if you're real. Searchable titles ("building a Shopify alternative in India"). Playlists per month/act.

**Cross-pollination:** Each YouTube video → 1 IG reel cut, 1 X thread, 1 LinkedIn reflection. One filming session feeds four platforms.

## 10. Trust-Building Strategy

Trust compounds from *consistency + honesty + specificity*.

1. **Show the stage honestly.** Saying "not launched, still on foundations" early inoculates you — no one can later accuse you of faking.
2. **Show your work, not just your wins.** Architecture diagrams, the bug that took 6 hours, the decision you reversed.
3. **Credit and learn in public.** "I was wrong about X" posts build more trust than any win.
4. **Keep promises small and kept.** "I'll share what I build this week" → then do, every week.
5. **Let people in gradually.** Month 1 you talk *to* them; Month 3 you build *with* them (feedback, waitlist, beta).

## 12. Tanglish Style Guide (Balanced)

Balanced = English backbone, natural Tamil phrases woven in. Reads like a real bilingual Chennai founder, not a translation.

**Per-platform intensity:**
| Platform | Tanglish level |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn | Mostly English; optional 1 Tanglish line for warmth |
| X | Mixed — English for insight tweets, Tanglish for raw/relatable ones |
| Instagram | Mixed-to-heavy in reels/captions (this is where it lands best) |
| YouTube | Mixed — title/desc English, spoken delivery naturally Tanglish |

**Do:**
- "Inniku almost 6 hours auth architecture fix panna pochu. One issue close panna, 10 hidden issues vandhuduchu."
- Keep technical/business *terms* in English (auth, checkout, merchant, UPI), connect with Tamil.

**Don't:**
- Force Tamil where it sounds unnatural.
- Translate idioms literally.
- Go heavy-Tanglish on LinkedIn (reads less professional to that audience).

**Reference pair (use as the calibration):**
> EN: *Today I spent 6 hours fixing authentication architecture.*
> TL: *Inniku almost 6 hours auth architecture fix panna pochu. Romba tough, but learning a lot.*

## 14. CTA Library

Match CTA to act:
- **Month 1 (grow):** "Follow the build." · "I'll be documenting all of this — stick around." · "What should I explain next?"
- **Month 2 (deepen):** "What would you build first?" · "Drop a 🔨 if you want the architecture breakdown." · "Tell me where I'm wrong."
- **Month 3 (convert):** "Want to be a first LetBuyy beta merchant? Comment 'beta'." · "Join the waitlist (link in bio)." · "If you've read this far, you're early. Welcome."

Never hard-sell. CTAs invite participation, not purchase.

## 15. What to Actually Track (no vanity, no fakery)

Track honestly, review monthly: followers per platform, post consistency (did you hit the rhythm?), comments/replies (conversation > reach), saves on carousels, watch-time on YouTube, waitlist signups (Month 3+). **Do not** post these numbers as flexes unless they tell a genuine story. The metric that matters most: *Did I show up every day this week?*
