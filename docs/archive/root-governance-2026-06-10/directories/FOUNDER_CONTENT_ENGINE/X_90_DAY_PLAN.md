# X / Twitter — 90 Day Plan

**Cadence:** 1–2 posts/day. AM = thought/lesson/journey. PM (optional) = build-log, micro-win, hot take, or reply-bait question.
**Voice on X:** mixed. English for insight/credibility tweets, Tanglish for raw/relatable ones.
**Format rules:** one idea per tweet · hook in the first line · no hashtags (they hurt reach here) · reply to every reply for the first 60 days · Sunday = recap thread (your weekly "episode").

> Month 1 = 30 fully-written daily anchor tweets + 4 Sunday recap threads + a second-tweet bank. Month 2 = adaptive system. Month 3 = journal-to-thread engine.

## WEEK 3 — The Why (Merchant Problem)

**Day 15 (EN):** An Indian merchant loses money in 4 invisible ways:
1. Settlement delay (cash lands days later)
2. Hidden fees (stacked until margin vanishes)
3. RTO on COD orders
4. No single source of truth
None of these show up as a scary dashboard number. That's why they're dangerous.

**Day 16 (TL):** UPI oru miracle — instant, free, everywhere. Acceptance solve aachu.
But merchant-oda real fear acceptance illa. **COD + returns** dhaan. Andha messy middle la dhaan naa focus pannren.

**Day 17 (EN):** Fee math nobody shows the merchant:
8% net margin. Platform takes 2% of the *sale*.
That 2% = a *quarter* of their profit. Gone. Before logistics. Before returns.
A fee is a % of the merchant's survival, not just the sale.

**Day 18 (TL):** RTO (return-to-origin) — silent killer.
Order ship aagudhu → customer reject → return varudhu. Packing + shipping + return shipping cost, revenue zero.
COD India la nalladhu, but idhu dhaan hidden bleed.

**Day 19 (EN):** The pattern I keep seeing in India commerce:
When a famous solution (UPI) nails one layer, the real opportunity moves to the *next* layer nobody's discussing.
UPI solved acceptance. The game is now trust + returns.

**Day 20 (TL):** Ketten oru merchant kitta: "Enna venum unakku?"
Expect pannen "more customers." Avar sonnaru: *"Enaku theriyanum — naa eththana keep panren-nu."*
Clarity > growth. Andha insight free-ah kedaikkadhu.

**Day 21 — SUNDAY RECAP THREAD (EN):**
Week 3: the merchant problem LetBuyy exists to solve 🧵
1/ 4 invisible money leaks: settlement delay, hidden fees, RTO returns, no single source of truth.
2/ UPI solved payment acceptance. It did NOT solve COD, trust, or returns. That's the real frontier.
3/ Fee reality: a 2% platform cut can be 25% of a small merchant's profit.
4/ The best product insight I got all week was one merchant sentence about *clarity*, not growth.
5/ Next week: the grind — college + loans + gym + startup, and my Month‑1 review.
This is the problem I can't stop thinking about. 🇮🇳

# MONTH 3 — JOURNAL → THREAD ENGINE

This is the engine you keep after Day 90. X becomes almost free to run.

## The daily micro-extraction (2 min)
Every night, lift **one** quotable line from your daily journal → schedule it as tomorrow's 8am tweet. Seven journals = seven days of X with zero extra writing.

## The Sunday thread compile
```
From my 7 daily journals (theme: <W9–W12>), build my Sunday recap thread (the weekly "episode"):
5–7 tweets, story arc, honest, ending on a "what happens next" hook that drives follows + waitlist.
Also pull 7 standalone quotable tweets for the week. Real numbers only.
```

## Month 3 X focus by week
| Week | Theme | The hook that matters |
|---|---|---|
| **W9** | Progress Recap | "90 days ago vs today" comparison tweets |
| **W10** | Toward Beta | "what I need to be ready for first merchants" |
