# Founder Vlog Plan — 12 Weekly Vlogs

**Publish:** Sundays (the "episode" of the week). **Length:** 5–10 min, raw and real.
**Difference from the main YouTube video:** the Friday video *teaches/explains*; the Sunday vlog *shows the life*. Less polish, more honesty. This is where people fall for the person, not the product.
**Per vlog:** Title · Hook · Thumbnail · Opening Scene · Middle Story · Ending · CTA.
**Tanglish:** spoken delivery naturally mixed/Tanglish — this is the most personal format.
**Anti-fake:** real days only. No staged wins. If the day was boring, show the boring.

# Vlog Formats Bank (rotate these for B-roll-light weeks)
Pull any of these when a week's main concept needs a lighter companion or you missed footage:
- **"Coding after work"** — the part-time-job-then-build grind, energy honesty.
- **"What I learned today"** — single-lesson micro-vlog, 3–4 min.
- **"Gym + startup life"** — the body/mind discipline that keeps you sane.
- **"Working on merchant experience"** — designing for a real user's anxiety.
- **"Learning from mistakes"** — one mistake, one lesson, raw.
- **"Financial struggles while building"** — honest money vlog (no glamorizing).
- **"Balancing college and startup"** — exam week chaos, triage in action.
- **"Weekly founder review"** — every non-monthly Sunday: 3 wins, 3 struggles, 1 lesson, next week's focus.

# Weekly Review structure (use every Sunday that isn't a monthly review)
```
1. This week's intent (callback to Sunday before)
2. 3 real things I did
3. 3 things that were hard
4. 1 lesson
5. Smallest real win
6. Next week's one focus
7. CTA: what should I show next?
```

# Production notes
- Shoot vertical clips too (reuse as IG reels / YT Shorts).
- Keep a consistent cold-open style so the vlog feels like a *series*.
- Always callback to a previous vlog ("remember the bug from last week?") — continuity is what makes people binge.
- Brand: lower-thirds and end card in violet `#8B5CF6`; end card = "Follow the build" + handles.
