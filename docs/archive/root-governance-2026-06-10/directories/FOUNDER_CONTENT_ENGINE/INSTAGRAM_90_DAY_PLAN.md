# Instagram — 90 Day Plan

**Cadence/week:** 2 Reels (Tue/Fri) · 2 Carousels (Wed/Sun) · Stories daily.
**Roles:** Reels = reach · Carousels = saves/credibility · Stories = intimacy/trust.
**Voice on IG:** mixed → heavy Tanglish (this is where it lands hardest). Always show your face in reels. Hook in the first 1.5 seconds.
**Per reel:** on-screen hook · shot list · script (EN + TL) · caption (EN + TL) · audio · Canva cover.
**Per carousel:** slide-by-slide · caption (EN + TL) · Canva template.

> Month 1 = 8 reels + 8 carousels + weekly story sets, fully written. Month 2 = adaptive. Month 3 = journal system.

# MONTH 3 — JOURNAL → IG SYSTEM

## The rule
Reels are emotion; carousels are proof. In Month 3, mine your daily journals for the **most emotional moment** (→ reel) and the **most useful lesson** (→ carousel) each week.

## Month 3 weekly prompt (IG slice)
```
From my 7 daily journals (theme: <W9–W12>), pull the single most emotional moment → 1 reel script,
and the single most useful lesson → 1 carousel. Add 1 "progress vs day 1" reel for the month.
EN + Tanglish. Surface a soft CTA toward the beta waitlist where natural.
```
