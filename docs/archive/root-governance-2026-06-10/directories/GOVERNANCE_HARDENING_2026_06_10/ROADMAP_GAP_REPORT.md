## 1. Executive verdict

**The roadmap is dependency-sound on its critical spine.** The longest hard-dependency chain (`§2.1`, `LETBUYY_FOUNDATION_ROADMAP.md:80-94`) — deploy-unblock → engage-RLS-off-`service_role` → ledger-the-capture → close-reconciliation → safe-PA-split-payout → hosted-origin → cart-handoff → beta — is correctly ordered: every required money/security/tenancy precedence the directive calls out is satisfied, and the P2↔P4 split (the spine's flagged trap) is stated correctly in three places. **All nine of the directive's required precedences are satisfied** (checklist §3). The roadmap is internally honest about the deploy keystone, the latent-money gate, and the "75%-complete-in-tree / 0%-live" caveat.

**However, the roadmap is NOT fully sound on (a) one genuine internal dependency cycle, (b) one missing-prerequisite producer for an exit-gate, and (c) the cross-document wave crosswalk, which silently disagrees with DOC 03 and DOC 04 on at least four items.** The roadmap folds the legacy plan's Waves 0–5 deletion schedule into a single phase (P5 = "Waves 3–5"), and in doing so flattens distinctions that DOC 04 holds as *separately gated* (the money-path retirements gated on Wave 1, the identity/runtime-composition retirements gated on Wave 2c). The result is a set of **wave-label mismatches** that, if an executor trusts the roadmap's P→Wave column over DOC 04's per-row wave, would mis-order or mis-gate specific deletions.

**Violation count: 14 findings** — **2 CRITICAL** (a dependency cycle that, taken literally, deadlocks the critical path; a money deletion whose roadmap gate is weaker than the constitutional/DOC-04 gate), **6 MAJOR** (missing-prerequisite producers, gate gaps, and an unstated split), **6 MINOR** (cross-doc wave mismatches). None of the CRITICAL findings is a strategy error — each is a fixable ordering/gate defect.

**Bottom line:** the roadmap's *narrative* sequencing is correct; its *machine-consumable* crosswalk (the P→Wave table at `:18-32`, the phase-gate table at `:613-627`) under-specifies the gates that DOC 04 and the Constitution make load-bearing. Fix the P8↔P5 DNS cycle, harden the P5/P3 deletion gates to match ART-24/ART-10, add the two missing producer-phases (the Liquid canary scaffolding owner; the `app_tenant`-role producer for P3/P8/P9 exits), and reconcile the six wave labels.

### ROAD-G-001 (CRITICAL) — P8 hosted origin hard-depends on a DNS record P5 produces later; the `checkout.` record must be split out of P5 and pulled forward to P8/P2-DNS

**The cycle.** P8 (Checkout, **Wave 2**) must build and serve `checkout.letbuyy.com` and is on the critical path *before* the beta gate (`:28`, `:89`, `:148`, `:492` "This is the final link of the critical path before the beta gate"). But the `checkout.` DNS record is listed as a **P5 deliverable** (Platform Simplification, **Wave 3**): `:404` ("the `letbuyy.com` DNS zone incl. the `checkout.` record (couples P8)") and the P8 side admits the dependency the other way: `:478` ("The hosted origin couples to the **P5 domain DNS** (the `checkout.` record)") and `:486` ("the `checkout.` DNS record (couples P5)"). The phase-gate table makes the contradiction explicit: P8's **entry-gate is "P2 kernel + P5 DNS"** (`:620`) while P8 sits in Wave 2 and P5 in Waves 3–5 (`:25`, `:28`). **A phase cannot have an entry-gate produced by a later phase.** Taken literally, P8 can never enter, and the critical path deadlocks.

**Why it is real, not cosmetic.** The full `letbuyy.store`→`letbuyy.com` domain migration is correctly a P5/Wave-3 event (cookie-domain flip couples identity D-07, 143 files, SSO-break risk — `:398`, DOC 04 `LEG-077`). But provisioning **one** apex/subdomain record (`checkout.letbuyy.com`) on the already-canonical `.com` zone has none of that risk and is a hard precondition for P8's exit. The mermaid graph even draws the safe direction for the handoff (`HANDOFF --> DOM`, `:184`) but not for the DNS the origin needs.

**EXACT FIX.** Split the `checkout.` DNS record out of the P5 domain-migration deliverable and make it a **P8 (Wave 2) sub-deliverable with its own entry-gate** ("`checkout.letbuyy.com` DNS record provisioned on the `.com` zone"), explicitly decoupled from the full `.store`→`.com` cutover. Reword P8's entry-gate (`:620`) from "P2 kernel + P5 DNS" to "**P2 kernel + the `checkout.` DNS record (provisioned in P8, not the full P5 domain migration)**." Keep the *full* domain migration (cookie scope, 301s, `.store` ban) in P5/Wave 3. Update `:404` to read "the `checkout.` record was provisioned in P8; P5 completes the apex migration and 301s." This removes the cycle without re-architecting anything.
