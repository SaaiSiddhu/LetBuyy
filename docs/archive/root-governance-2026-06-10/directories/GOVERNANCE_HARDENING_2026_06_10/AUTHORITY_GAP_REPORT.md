## 1. Executive verdict

**Is the authority chain sound? — Largely, but not airtight. The *spine* is sound; the *encoding* leaks.**

The intended hierarchy is correctly and consistently stated as a **direction** in all 8 documents: the Constitution is supreme in every doc that references authority, no subordinate doc claims supremacy, and no subordinate doc claims to supersede something it should not. The ranks assigned to docs 1–6 are internally consistent across the stack. That is the good news, and it is the load-bearing part: an agent reading any one doc will correctly conclude "Constitution wins."

The breaks are in the **mechanics of the encoding**, and they are real:

- **The chain is defined N times, in N documents, sourced from a document the chain outranks.** Every doc restates the 6-rank hierarchy with its own wording, and every doc attributes that hierarchy to **`_GOVERNANCE_SPINE.md` §S2** — a file in the rank-6 evidence base. The supreme law's structure is therefore *cited from* a document it is supposed to outrank. No doc declares which restatement is canonical.
- **AGENTS_V2 still tells agents to treat V1 + Freeze as the live constitution** "until DOC 01 physically exists" — but DOC 01 exists now. Three separate clauses point agents at retired evidence as live authority. This is the single most dangerous finding: it actively routes an agent away from the supreme law the Constitution claims to be.
- **The stack is "7 documents" in its own self-description but 8 on disk.** `LETBUYY_FINAL_ARCHITECTURE_SCORECARD.md` is DOC 07, lives in the folder, and is referenced by the Index — yet the numbered authority chain (ranks 1–6) has no slot for it, and the Index files it under a non-rank label "reference."
- **A runtime-name inconsistency (`services/` vs `apps/identity-platform`)** sits in the rank-2 rulebook's canonical-topology table, disagreeing with the rank-1 Constitution and the rank-3 registry.
- **The ops-console survivor is an unmade decision the chain never forces to closure** — flagged UNVERIFIED in all 8 docs, owned by the ARB per §12.2.5, but with no single recorded "decision pending here" pointer, so it reads as 8 parallel tentative guesses.
- **A ratification-status contradiction:** the Constitution's header declares itself **"RATIFIED. SUPREME."** while its own §14.2 and the Index, Roadmap P0, and AGENTS_V2 all describe ratification as a *future ARB act* ("govern by directive until…"). A reader cannot tell from the documents whether the Constitution is live or pending.

**Break count: 14 authority findings — 3 CRITICAL, 5 MAJOR, 6 MINOR.** None require an architecture change; all are surgical doc edits. The chain's *intent* is sound; its *self-consistency* needs hardening before it can be relied on as machine-enforceable law.

## 3. Per-issue detail (the reasoning behind each finding)

### 3.1 The chain is restated in 8 places and sourced from the document it outranks (AUTH-G-006)

The intended hierarchy appears, in full or in part, in: Index L9–15; Constitution P.3 L30–41; AGENTS_V2 `AUTH-CHAIN` L26–41; Canonical Systems §0.1 L17–24; Legacy §0 L13; Roadmap Status L6 + P0 L243; UI/UX §0 L14; Scorecard L4. Eight restatements, each with slightly different wording (the Index says "higher wins"; AGENTS_V2 says "the lower ordinal wins (1 is supreme)" L28; the Constitution says "the higher authority wins" L34). The wordings do not *contradict* — they agree on order — but there is **no canonical statement**, so a future edit to one will silently diverge from the other seven, which is exactly the "two/three/four-constitution" drift the Constitution P.3 (L32) says it exists to end.

Worse, six of the eight (Canonical Systems L15, Legacy L13, Roadmap L6, UI/UX L14, Scorecard L4, and by extension any doc that "traces to" it) attribute the chain to **`_GOVERNANCE_SPINE.md` §S2**. That file is in the **rank-6 evidence base** (the Constitution P.3 item 6, L39, names `STRATEGIC_CANONICALIZATION_2026_06_10/` as "the 'why,' not authority"; `_GOVERNANCE_SPINE.md` lives there). So the **authority hierarchy itself is cited from a document the hierarchy ranks last.** This is the inversion the whole rewrite condemns: deriving law from the evidence it is supposed to outrank. The Constitution alone avoids it — P.3 restates the chain on its own authority and does not cite the spine — which is precisely why the Constitution should be named the **sole canonical source of the chain**, and every other doc should *reference Constitution §P.3*, not re-derive from the spine.

This is not circular in the strict A→B→A sense, but it is an **authority-source inversion** and a **duplication-without-a-canonical-owner**, both of which DOC-R4 (AGENTS_V2 L305, "One source of truth per fact… update the owner") forbids for ordinary facts and which must apply a fortiori to the authority chain.

### 3.2 The stack is "7 documents" but 8 exist; DOC 07 has no rank (AUTH-G-002)

The Index §"The 7 documents" (L26) and its table (L27–35) enumerate seven, then **list an eighth row** — `LETBUYY_FINAL_ARCHITECTURE_SCORECARD.md`, labeled "reference" (L35). The Roadmap P0 goal says "Ratify the **seven** governance documents" (L243). But on disk there are **8 files**, and the SCORECARD's own header calls it "**DOC 07 of the Governance Rewrite**" (L3) with an authority position "subordinate to DOC 01/02/03" (L4). So the stack simultaneously: (a) calls itself 7 docs, (b) ships 8, (c) numbers the 8th "DOC 07," and (d) gives it the non-rank label "reference" that does not appear anywhere in the rank-1–6 chain. An agent asked "what is the SCORECARD's authority?" gets three different answers depending on which line it reads. The Scorecard is genuinely *reference* (it scores, it does not rule — its own L4 says so), so the fix is to **formally seat "reference / rank-6-adjacent" in the chain definition** and correct the "seven" counts to "eight (one of which — DOC 07 — is reference-rank)."

### 3.3 AGENTS_V2 routes agents to retired evidence as live authority (AUTH-G-001 — the worst finding)

AGENTS_V2 was authored when DOC 01/DOC 03 did not yet physically exist, and it left three "until they exist, follow V1+Freeze" bridges in place:

- **AUTH-R3 (L41):** "Until DOC 01 physically exists in the tree, follow V1+Freeze directly as the constitution and this file as DOC 02."
- **Appendix B (L402):** "`LETBUYY_CONSTITUTION.md` (DOC 01) and `LETBUYY_CANONICAL_SYSTEMS.md` (DOC 03) are the named authorities… until they physically exist, follow V1+Freeze as the constitution and the `STRATEGIC_CANONICALIZATION_2026_06_10/` winner registry… as DOC 03."

DOC 01 and DOC 03 **now physically exist** (they are in the same folder as AGENTS_V2, read 2026-06-10). The Constitution's entire premise (P.2, L20–28; ART-01, L145; §15, L469) is that it **absorbs V1 + Freeze and retires them to `docs/archive/` as non-authority history**. So these clauses now instruct an agent to do the one thing the Constitution most forbids: **treat the retired V1/Freeze as the live constitution.** This is a direct contradiction of ART-01's "Forbids: Citing `V1`, `FREEZE`… as a competing authority" (L148) and of AGENTS_V2's own AUTH-R2 (L40, "MUST NOT cite… any `docs/archive/*` document as current authority"). The condition the bridges were waiting on has been met; the bridges are now live wrong-turns and must be cut.

### 3.4 The ops-console survivor: an unmade decision the chain never forces (AUTH-G-007 — seed b)

Across all 8 documents, *which* internal ops console survives consolidation is flagged **UNVERIFIED** and tentatively named `platform-console`:

- Constitution ART-24 (L302): "which one survives is an ARB decision (UNVERIFIED, tentatively `platform-console`)"; §14.4 (L457).
- AGENTS_V2 ARCH-R10 (L95): "the survivor is an **ARB decision, UNVERIFIED**, do not hard-code the loser as canonical"; Appendix B (L400).
- Canonical Systems §2 #27 (L98), §3.7 (L259), §7.1 (L473).
- Legacy LEG-022/023 (L129–130), §6.1 (L368).
- Roadmap R5.5 (L401), §5 UNVERIFIED #6 (L659).
- Scorecard (rolls into Merchant OS / Mobile, L35).

The **authority to decide it is clearly located** — the Constitution §12.2 item 5 (L371) gives the ARB power to "Resolve `UNVERIFIED` items (§14.4) once verification evidence exists," and §11.2 item 6 (L349) forbids an agent from silently upgrading an UNVERIFIED item to fact. So the *governance* is correct: it is an ARB Class-B decision (Constitution §11.1, L338). **The gap is procedural, not authority:** there is no single place that says "**this specific decision is open; record the resolution HERE.**" It is restated as a tentative guess in 8 places, so the eventual resolution has 8 spots to update and no canonical home — the same duplication defect as AUTH-G-006, applied to one decision. The fix is not to *make* the decision (that would violate §11.2.6) but to give it **one decision-record home** — e.g. a one-line "ARB Decision Register" stub in the Constitution §14.4 or the Legacy plan §6.1 — that the other 7 mentions reference instead of re-stating tentatively.

### 3.5 "RATIFIED. SUPREME." vs "becomes live upon ratification" (AUTH-G-004)

The Constitution's header status (L8) reads **"RATIFIED. SUPREME. NOTHING OVERRIDES IT."** and §15 (L465) says the ARB "ratifies the LetBuyy Constitution as SUPREME and effective 2026-06-10." But:

- Constitution §14.2 (L438–447): "This Constitution becomes **live and operative** upon the ARB's ratification, which simultaneously [promotes/demotes/archives/directs]… **Until the ARB executes §14.2**, humans and agents follow this Constitution **by directive**."
- Index L40: "These documents govern 'by directive' until the ARB executes the ratification act. **GATE 1 — ratify the governance.**"
- Roadmap P0 (L243, L254): "**Ratify** the seven governance documents"; exit criteria "ARB minutes signed" (L260) — framed as not-yet-done.
- AGENTS_V2 header (L5): "amended only by a human ARB decision" and the memory/index both record AGENTS_V2 as **"NOT yet promoted to root (pending ARB GATE-1 ratification)."**

So the documents disagree with **themselves** on whether ratification has happened: the Constitution's masthead says it is a done deal; its own amendment section and three sibling docs say it is the pending GATE-1 act. This is a genuine contradiction about the single most important status in the stack — *is the supreme law live or proposed?* The fix is to reconcile the masthead with §14.2: either (a) state "RATIFIED BY DIRECTIVE; operative-ratification (§14.2 archival + validator-retarget) PENDING GATE-1," or (b) if the ARB has in fact executed §14.2, update the Index/Roadmap to match. Given the memory record ("AGENTS_V2 NOT yet promoted… pending ARB GATE-1") and Constitution §12.3 (L374, promotion "reserved to the ARB"), option (a) is the accurate reconciliation.

### 3.6 AGENTS_V2 "Supersedes the previous root AGENTS.md" reads as accomplished, but promotion is pending (AUTH-G-003)

AGENTS_V2's header (L7) states flatly: "**AGENTS_V2 replaces it.**" and "**Supersedes:** the previous root `AGENTS.md`." But the operative root `AGENTS.md` is **not yet replaced** — promotion is a constitutional act reserved to the ARB at GATE-1 (Constitution §12.3 L374; Index L42 "Promote `LETBUYY_AGENTS_V2.md` → the operative `AGENTS.md`" as a ratification-path step). Until GATE-1, the **live root `AGENTS.md` remains the drifted generated file** (the very `CLAUDE.md` in the repo root still points agents at the old generated authority). AGENTS_V2's header tense asserts a *completed* supersession that has not occurred. This is an over-claim of *operative status* (not of authority rank — its rank-2 claim is correct). The fix is to tense it as **pending**: "supersedes the previous root `AGENTS.md` **upon ARB promotion at GATE-1**; until then the live root `AGENTS.md` remains the generated file and this document governs by directive." This keeps AGENTS_V2 honest about the gap between "authored" and "operative," which is the exact distinction ANTI-R11 (L324) and DOC-R3 (L304) demand of every other artifact.

### 3.7 The runtime-name inconsistency: `services/identity-platform` vs `apps/identity-platform` (AUTH-G-005 — seed a)

In the rank-2 rulebook's canonical-topology table, row 4 reads:

> AGENTS_V2 L73: `| 4 | services/identity-platform *(app root: apps/identity-platform)* | Issuer satellite | …`

But the rank-1 Constitution and the rank-3 registry name it as an **app**:

- Constitution ART-04 topology (L166): "`identity-platform`" (bare, grouped with the satellites but with no `services/` prefix); the runtime map in `CLAUDE.md` and the canonical one-liner treat it as `apps/identity-platform`.
- Canonical Systems §2 row 3 (L74): "**`apps/identity-platform`** = sole issuer"; §3.1 (L119): "Winner: **`apps/identity-platform`**."

Only the **app** exists on disk (the memory record confirms "only the app exists"; `services/identity-platform` is not a real runtime root). AGENTS_V2's own parenthetical "*(app root: `apps/identity-platform`)*" concedes this — the row is self-contradictory: it leads with the wrong path and corrects itself in a parenthetical. Because AGENTS_V2 is the doc an agent reads to learn the topology, the **leading** `services/identity-platform` is the string that will be copied into manifests, deploy matrices, and import paths. This is a rank-1-vs-rank-2 disagreement on a canonical runtime name — small in bytes, load-bearing in effect (it is exactly the class of "estate lies about itself" ANTI-R7, L320, forbids). The fix is to make AGENTS_V2 L73 read `apps/identity-platform` as the primary, and add a one-line note that the "4 satellites" framing is **logical** (identity-platform is the issuer satellite *role*) not **physical** (it is a Vercel/Next app root, not a `services/` worker), so the logical-vs-physical mismatch that produced the wrong prefix is documented and cannot recur.

### 3.8 Doc-number vs rank mapping is never defined (AUTH-G-008)

Documents are referenced two ways — by **doc-number** ("DOC 01"…"DOC 07") and by **rank** ("rank 1"…"rank 5"/"reference"). For docs 1–3 the number equals the rank, so the ambiguity is invisible. But it diverges at the bottom: **DOC 04 + DOC 05 are both rank 4**; **DOC 06 is rank 5**; **DOC 07 is "reference" (no numbered rank)**. UI/UX explicitly calls itself "DOC 06 — UI/UX Governance" (L3) and "Level 5" (L14) in the same document without stating that the two numbering systems differ. No doc defines the mapping, so "DOC 06" and "rank 6" (which is the *evidence base*, not UI/UX) are a live collision waiting to confuse. The fix is a one-line mapping note in the Index and the Constitution §P.3: "Doc-numbers (DOC 01–07) are stable identifiers; authority **ranks** are 1–6 where DOC 04+05 share rank 4, DOC 06 is rank 5, DOC 07 is reference (rank-6-adjacent)."

### 3.9 What is NOT broken (the chain's genuine strengths — recorded so they are not "fixed")

- **Supremacy is unambiguous and universal.** Every doc that references authority names the Constitution as supreme; none claims the apex; none claims to supersede the Constitution. (Constitution L34/L471; AGENTS_V2 L33; Canonical L19; Legacy L13; Roadmap L6; UI/UX L14; Scorecard L4.)
- **No subordinate doc makes a constitutional-level decision.** The registry (DOC 03) explicitly says it "does not re-litigate the winners" (L11) and is "bound by DOC 01/02" (L26); the plans (DOC 04/05) explicitly defer "which system" to DOC 03 and "is it canonical / may I expand scope" to DOC 01 (Legacy L13; Roadmap L6); UI/UX is "supreme on all UI/UX matters **below** [the higher docs]" (L14) — a correctly *scoped* supremacy, not a global one. No over-claim of *rank* exists; the only over-claims found are of *status* (AUTH-G-003, AUTH-G-004), not authority.
- **No strict circular reference exists.** No doc names a lower-ranked doc as *its* superior. (The closest is the chain-source inversion of AUTH-G-006, which is an authority-*source* problem, not a rank cycle.)
- **Every doc has a supersession/authority header.** There is no doc *missing* a chain-position statement; the defects are in the *content* of those headers, not their absence. (AUTH-G-009 below is the one partial-header case — the Scorecard.)
