# LETBUYY GOVERNANCE HARDENING — FORENSIC REVIEW (2026-06-10)

> **What this is:** a deep governance forensic review of the 7-document governance stack produced in `GOVERNANCE_REWRITE_2026_06_10/`. The goal was NOT more documents or new architecture — it was to hunt the seams that let drift back in: contradictions, duplicate authority, ambiguity, and rules that say "MUST" with no machine behind them. Nine reports; the architecture was not touched and the 7 governance docs were not modified (verified: untouched, mtimes predate this run).
> **Stage 4 of the program** — after the forensic audit (problems), canonicalization (winners), and the governance rewrite (the law), this is the audit *of the law itself*.

---

## The one-sentence verdict
**LetBuyy now has excellent governance law that is almost entirely not yet in force** — document quality ≈ **8.4/10**, but in-force enforcement ≈ **2.5/10** and weighted governance-maturity ≈ **3.7/10** — because the live repo still runs the *old* drifted `AGENTS.md` and ~70–88 "theater" validators that certify the rejected architecture, and the act that would fix this (GATE-1) is **mechanically self-blocking**.

## The single thing that matters most: GATE-1 is self-blocking
Promoting `LETBUYY_AGENTS_V2.md` to the live `AGENTS.md` **fails CI** — because `repository-intelligence-validator` hard-asserts that the *drifted* AGENTS.md strings (the 9-app/CQRS topology) must be present, and `runtime-manifest-validator` fails any manifest that doesn't delegate to the rejected packages, and `governance-common.mjs` *is* the 9-app truth-source. The law cannot come into force through the existing gate. **HW-0 fixes this as one atomic commit:** promote AGENTS_V2 → `AGENTS.md` (strip the `GENERATED BY` header) **and in the same commit** retarget those three drift-locking validators to the V1 8-runtime map, delete the ~70 theater validators alongside their dead packages, retire the scan-generator, and point `CLAUDE.md` at AGENTS_V2. Until HW-0 lands, every other fix is inert and an agent reading the repo is actively taught the wrong architecture.

## When is Codex actually drift-safe?
Four tiers (from the hardening plan): (1) the live `AGENTS.md` *is* AGENTS_V2 and the validators stop requiring the rejected packages; (2) the gate *fails* an agent that posts outside the ledger, defaults a fee, custodies funds, mints a token outside identity-platform, or writes a `USING(true)` anon policy; (3) it fails the cardinal sin (a parallel second system), React growth, and live-extension wiring; (4) the chain is single-sourced, complete, and the 4 forced decisions are resolved. **Honest residual:** "a parallel second system" is only partially machine-detectable — it rides on an escalation rule — and live-DB state needs deploy-lane probes. Hardened means *every mechanizable path is blocked and every non-mechanizable one has a named escalation or live probe*, not that agent goodwill is eliminated.
