## 0. Methodology

1. **Read all 7 phase reports in full** and extracted every finding ID, severity, and "path-to-fix." Tally: P1 = 14 (AUTH-G-001…014), P2 = 9 (CANON-G-001…009), P3 = 18 (DUP-G-001…018), P4 = 18 (LEGV-G-001…018), P5 = 14 (ROAD-G-001…014), P6 = 14 (CODEX-G-001…014), P7 = 42 (VAL-G-001…042). **Sum = 129.**
2. **Defined 8 governance dimensions** (per the brief) and bound each to the phase report(s) that produced its evidence.
3. **Scored each dimension twice** — a **document-quality score** (is the *written law* good?) and an **in-force score** (is the law *actually enforced on an agent today?*). This split is the headline; it is reported as a two-column view in §3.
4. **Computed a weighted overall** with the weights the brief mandates — **Codex Safety, Drift Resistance, and Authority weighted heaviest** (they are the point of a governance *system*; a law nobody is bound by is a draft, not a government).
5. **Scoring rubric (0–10):** 10 = complete, self-consistent, single-sourced, and machine-enforced with zero residual drift. 8–9 = strong, minor hygiene. 6–7 = sound in intent, real gaps. 4–5 = partial; load-bearing holes. 2–3 = exists but largely inoperative. 0–1 = absent or actively inverted (the machine enforces the opposite).
6. **Every score cites the finding IDs that hold it down.** No score is asserted without the blocking evidence.

**The one distinction this whole scorecard turns on:** the **7 documents are excellent** (the written law is coherent, single-chain, winner-complete, gated, and honest about its own gaps), but the **enforcement is near-zero and in places inverted** (the file an agent reads and the suite that gates it both encode the *rejected* architecture, and money/identity have zero machine backing). Scores for *document quality* are therefore high where due; scores for *Codex-safety / drift-resistance* are floor-level — and **that gap, not any single document defect, is the verdict.**

## 5. The single blocking gap that most depresses the overall

**GATE-1 is not executed — and it is mechanically self-blocked — so the entire enforcement layer is inert while the live machine actively enforces the rejected architecture.**

This one compound gap is the keystone. It has three interlocking faces, each a CRITICAL finding, and together they cap dimensions 6 and 7 (the heavily-weighted ones) at ~1:

1. **The new law is invisible.** The file an agent reads (`CLAUDE.md` → root `AGENTS.md`, the 2026-06-08 scan-generated drift) declares the 9-app/CQRS topology canonical; AGENTS_V2 is referenced by **zero** files an agent traverses (CODEX-G-001; P6 §"governance-is-not-yet-live"). Discoverability of the new law on a normal agent run ≈ 0%.
2. **The machine is inverted, not merely absent.** `runtime-manifest-validator` *fails CI unless* manifests delegate to the rejected packages (CODEX-G-002); `governance-common.mjs` *is* the 9-app truth-source (CODEX-G-003); `architecture-validator` *requires* the dead packages to exist (VAL-G-015); ~70 theater validators certify a forbidden distributed system so "green" means "conforms to what the company rejected" (VAL-G-018). An agent that obeys the repository **builds and re-promotes the wrong architecture.**
3. **Promotion is trapped by itself.** `repository-intelligence-validator` *hard-asserts the drift strings must be present* in `AGENTS.md` (CODEX-G-001) — so the act of swapping in the correct law turns CI red. GATE-1 cannot be executed by editing one file; it requires retargeting that validator + `governance-common.mjs` + `runtime-manifest-validator` **in the same commit**, with the ~70 fiction validators deleted alongside their dead packages.

**Compounding it (the highest catastrophe-to-effort sub-gap): money has zero enforcement.** Independent of GATE-1, the strongest prose in the Constitution (ART-10/11/12/13) has **no validator at all** — `commission_bps` (VAL-G-004 / CODEX-G-005), ledger-writer (VAL-G-002), `razorpayx`/custody (VAL-G-005), mutable-balance (VAL-G-003), provider-mode badge (VAL-G-013) all grep to NONE. A capture path can post nowhere, default a 3% rake, or custody funds with a fully green gate — and the roadmap itself flags these S1s as "one config flip from active loss." These four money validators are the cheapest large risk-cut available and **can ship before promotion**.

**Why this is *the* gap and not any document defect:** every other finding in the 129 is a *surgical doc edit* (re-point a citation, cut a stale clause, add a crosswalk table, seat the 8th doc). They improve the law's self-consistency but change *nothing* an agent experiences. This gap is the only one whose closure flips the machine from enforcing the wrong architecture to enforcing the right one — it is, in the words of the Constitution's own ART-02, "the act that makes this Constitution's words enforceable for the first time."
