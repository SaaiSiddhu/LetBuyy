## Reproducible safe future inspection protocol

1. Obtain a fresh bounded Chrome allocation and direct authorization accepted by automatic review. Record theme name, version, market, clean/dirty state, time, viewport and screenshot ID without private data or signed URLs.
2. Open only existing non-mutating pickers and menus. For each category, record trigger, menu location, options, disabled/empty states, focus/Escape and selected template/resource/URL/tree. Do not assign a template.
3. Select existing section/block rows and inspector outlines. Record selected IDs/labels, panel contents, hover/insertion controls, and measured canvas width. Do not change values or structure.
4. Search catalogs, then dismiss. Record whether Custom Liquid and app blocks are offered, but do not add either.
5. Leave Save, Publish, Undo, duplicate, CSS/Liquid editing, resource assignments and all persistent actions unexercised. Mark their result as documented or **NOT VERIFIED**.

Public documented behavior and source URLs are in [Shopify public contracts](SHOPIFY_PUBLIC_CONTRACTS.md). Shopify's private implementation, sanitizer, storage and network protocols were not inspected.
