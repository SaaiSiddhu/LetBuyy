# Savor — current free-theme dossier

Research: 22 October 2026. This is a comparative expert inspection, not user testing or a certification.

## Positioning and evidence boundary

**DOCUMENTED:** [Official listing](https://themes.shopify.com/themes/savor/presets/savor); developer Shopify; free catalog entry; version 4.3.0, dated 9 October 2026. Family: Horizon collection — explicitly identified in listing copy. Available catalog preset inspected: `savor`; no alternate preset was verified. The listing's industry cross-links are discovery links, not an exclusive niche constraint. [Listing capture](../evidence/savor-listing.json) separates overview, feature marketing and release text.

**OBSERVED / INFERRED:** The demo presents **sauces / specialty food** through a **flavor-led product education** approach. Merchant fit and customer intent are inferred from catalog/content; demo pricing is not proof of the merchants who will succeed with it. The catalog-size assumptions below concern the inspected sample, not a platform limit.

## Information architecture and homepage

Primary observed entry vocabulary: **Shop / About / Recipes**. Search/cart are utility entries rather than the main editorial message. Footer link groups and subscription content are recorded in [route text](../evidence/savor-route-summary.json); forms were not submitted. Desktop and mobile navigation differ in available width; mobile hides the full category row behind the menu where provided. Captured opened menu.

Ordered synthesis of the homepage: **Bold condiment hero → flavor range → featured packs → founder/family story → recipes**. This is a functional zone sequence, not an exhaustive inventory of every block. The [full-page desktop capture](../evidence/savor-home-desktop.jpg) includes below-fold content; the [comparison sheet](../visuals/inspection-sheets/savor.jpg) intentionally crops to the opening viewport. Photography and demo copy contribute to identity and can be changed independently of template structure.

## Product, cart and mobile purchase sequence

Bottle-led gallery with pack-size choices; quantity and purchase beside ingredients and preparation context. Observed option-group text: Bottle Pack Size / 1 Bottle / 2 Bottles / 3 Bottles / 6 Bottles. Product-image alt text and available stock labels are content, not validated certification. On narrow screens the sequence must be judged from [mobile product evidence](../evidence/savor-product-mobile.jpg), not an editor's device frame. Gallery/detail ordering in that capture is the observed state; sticky behavior during arbitrary scroll positions is not exhaustively verified.

Captured empty-cart entry. No purchase, checkout commitment or order was made. Empty-cart presentation is established; successful cart mutation, discount application, checkout, payment and fulfillment are **untested**. Related-product regions may load after the first capture; a visible placeholder is not proof recommendations completed.

## Visual system and measurement

Barlow Condensed with Inter; saturated red, cream and dark text; assertive typography. Computed body font declaration: `Inter, sans-serif`; first substantial heading declaration: `"Barlow Condensed", sans-serif`. These are computed CSS family lists, not a network/font-file audit. Recorded body background: `rgb(255, 255, 255)`. [Geometry/style JSON](../evidence/savor-home-desktop.json) and, where present, product role measurements preserve individual sizes, spacing, borders and radii. A page background is not an entire semantic palette.

Actual comparison conditions: desktop **1440 × 1000 CSS px**, mobile **390 × 844**, DPR **1**. Native storefront viewports were used for final captures. No claim is made that a screenshot establishes keyboard, screen-reader, reduced-motion or performance compliance. Hover/focus and all timing stages remain incompletely tested.

## Tradeoffs and transferable lessons

Flavor language needs explicit ingredient/allergen data; a purchase action alone cannot tell dietary suitability. This is an expert hypothesis about fit/friction, not demonstrated conversion impact. The opening screenshot may show images still loading; compare mobile and role measurements before treating a blank frame as intended design.

**LetBuyy implication:** Transfer pack-size clarity and recipe-to-product links; no invented nutritional or allergen claims. Shared add-to-cart, stock and hosted checkout contracts remain shared. Borrow a task-support principle; do not reproduce Shopify assets, copy, private behavior or an entire composition. Listed app/B2B capabilities are not automatically present in LetBuyy.
