# Crave — current free-theme dossier

Research: 22 October 2026. This is a comparative expert inspection, not user testing or a certification.

## Positioning and evidence boundary

**DOCUMENTED:** [Official listing](https://themes.shopify.com/themes/crave/presets/crave); developer Shopify; free catalog entry; version 16.1.0, dated 9 October 2026. Family: Earlier section-theme cohort; shared source ancestry not established in this audit. Available catalog preset inspected: `crave`; no alternate preset was verified. The listing's industry cross-links are discovery links, not an exclusive niche constraint. [Listing capture](../evidence/crave-listing.json) separates overview, feature marketing and release text.

**OBSERVED / INFERRED:** The demo presents **pantry meal starters** through a **playful preparation and flavor** approach. Proza Libre and Archivo; cream, maroon and vivid photography; outlined pill controls. Computed body font declaration: `Archivo, sans-serif`; first substantial heading declaration: `"Proza Libre", sans-serif`. These are computed CSS family lists, not a network/font-file audit. [Geometry/style JSON](../evidence/crave-home-desktop.json) and, where present, product role measurements preserve individual sizes, spacing, borders and radii. A page background is not an entire semantic palette.

Actual comparison conditions: desktop **1440 × 1000 CSS px**, mobile **390 × 844**, DPR **1**. Native storefront viewports were used for final captures. No claim is made that a screenshot establishes keyboard, screen-reader, reduced-motion or performance compliance. Hover/focus and all timing stages remain incompletely tested.

## Tradeoffs and transferable lessons

Some desktop media was still blank while the mobile image loaded; preparation copy must expose allergens without unnecessary digging. This is an expert hypothesis about fit/friction, not demonstrated conversion impact. The opening screenshot may show images still loading; compare mobile and role measurements before treating a blank frame as intended design.

**LetBuyy implication:** Transfer meal context plus explicit pack quantities; keep allergen information before commitment, without promising rapid delivery. Shared add-to-cart, stock and hosted checkout contracts remain shared. Borrow a task-support principle; do not reproduce Shopify assets, copy, private behavior or an entire composition. Listed app/B2B capabilities are not automatically present in LetBuyy.
