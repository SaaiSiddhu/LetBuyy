## Tradeoffs and transferable lessons

Small cards can understate product scale; dimensions deserve explicit context for unfamiliar home objects. This is an expert hypothesis about fit/friction, not demonstrated conversion impact. The opening screenshot may show images still loading; compare mobile and role measurements before treating a blank frame as intended design.

**LetBuyy implication:** Transfer scale/material information and restrained object presentation; room-scale furniture needs a more deliberate measurement zone. Shared add-to-cart, stock and hosted checkout contracts remain shared. Borrow a task-support principle; do not reproduce Shopify assets, copy, private behavior or an entire composition. Listed app/B2B capabilities are not automatically present in LetBuyy.
