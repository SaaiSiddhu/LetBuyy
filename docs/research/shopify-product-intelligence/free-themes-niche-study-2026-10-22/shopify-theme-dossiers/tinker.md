## Tradeoffs and transferable lessons

Decor and chargers have different information requirements; technical compatibility cannot be inferred from a desk setting. This is an expert hypothesis about fit/friction, not demonstrated conversion impact. The opening screenshot may show images still loading; compare mobile and role measurements before treating a blank frame as intended design.

**LetBuyy implication:** Transfer task-based navigation and compact dimension tiles; separate electronics compatibility from decoration. Shared add-to-cart, stock and hosted checkout contracts remain shared. Borrow a task-support principle; do not reproduce Shopify assets, copy, private behavior or an entire composition. Listed app/B2B capabilities are not automatically present in LetBuyy.
