## Visual system and measurement

Cormorant serif; white gallery space and restrained accent; centered curatorial text. Computed body font declaration: `Cormorant, serif`; first substantial heading declaration: `Cormorant, serif`. These are computed CSS family lists, not a network/font-file audit. Recorded body background: `rgb(255, 255, 255)`. [Geometry/style JSON](../evidence/studio-home-desktop.json) and, where present, product role measurements preserve individual sizes, spacing, borders and radii. A page background is not an entire semantic palette.

Actual comparison conditions: desktop **1440 × 1000 CSS px**, mobile **390 × 844**, DPR **1**. Native storefront viewports were used for final captures. No claim is made that a screenshot establishes keyboard, screen-reader, reduced-motion or performance compliance. Hover/focus and all timing stages remain incompletely tested.

## Tradeoffs and transferable lessons

Artwork proportions must remain intact; generic collection headings can lose creator context; gift-card workflow does not establish art option support. This is an expert hypothesis about fit/friction, not demonstrated conversion impact. The opening screenshot may show images still loading; compare mobile and role measurements before treating a blank frame as intended design.

**LetBuyy implication:** Transfer author/creator discovery for books and craft; keep gift cards separate from ordinary product variants. Shared add-to-cart, stock and hosted checkout contracts remain shared. Borrow a task-support principle; do not reproduce Shopify assets, copy, private behavior or an entire composition. Listed app/B2B capabilities are not automatically present in LetBuyy.
