# Sense — current free-theme dossier

Research: 22 October 2026. This is a comparative expert inspection, not user testing or a certification.

## Positioning and evidence boundary

**DOCUMENTED:** [Official listing](https://themes.shopify.com/themes/sense/presets/sense); developer Shopify; free catalog entry; version 16.1.0, dated 9 October 2026. Family: Earlier section-theme cohort; shared source ancestry not established in this audit. Available catalog preset inspected: `sense`; no alternate preset was verified. The listing's industry cross-links are discovery links, not an exclusive niche constraint. [Listing capture](../evidence/sense-listing.json) separates overview, feature marketing and release text.

**OBSERVED / INFERRED:** The demo presents **skincare / personal care** through a **soft educational beauty** approach. Merchant fit and customer intent are inferred from catalog/content; demo pricing is not proof of the merchants who will succeed with it. The catalog-size assumptions below concern the inspected sample, not a platform limit.

## Visual system and measurement

Poppins; pale green/cream gradients, lavender accents and rounded overlap; medium airy density. Computed body font declaration: `Poppins, sans-serif`; first substantial heading declaration: `Poppins, sans-serif`. These are computed CSS family lists, not a network/font-file audit. Recorded body background: `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)`. [Geometry/style JSON](../evidence/sense-home-desktop.json) and, where present, product role measurements preserve individual sizes, spacing, borders and radii. A page background is not an entire semantic palette.

Actual comparison conditions: desktop **1440 × 1000 CSS px**, mobile **390 × 844**, DPR **1**. Native storefront viewports were used for final captures. No claim is made that a screenshot establishes keyboard, screen-reader, reduced-motion or performance compliance. Hover/focus and all timing stages remain incompletely tested.

## Tradeoffs and transferable lessons

Soft palettes still need control contrast; beauty imagery is not evidence of efficacy or suitability. This is an expert hypothesis about fit/friction, not demonstrated conversion impact. The opening screenshot may show images still loading; compare mobile and role measurements before treating a blank frame as intended design.

**LetBuyy implication:** Transfer a clear base-versus-applicator choice and ingredient education; reject automatic clinical claims. Shared add-to-cart, stock and hosted checkout contracts remain shared. Borrow a task-support principle; do not reproduce Shopify assets, copy, private behavior or an entire composition. Listed app/B2B capabilities are not automatically present in LetBuyy.
