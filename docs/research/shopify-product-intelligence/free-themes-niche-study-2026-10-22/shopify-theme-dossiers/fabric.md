# Fabric — current free-theme dossier

Research: 22 October 2026. This is a comparative expert inspection, not user testing or a certification.

## Positioning and evidence boundary

**DOCUMENTED:** [Official listing](https://themes.shopify.com/themes/fabric/presets/fabric); developer Shopify; free catalog entry; version 4.3.0, dated 9 October 2026. Family: Horizon collection — explicitly identified in listing copy. Available catalog preset inspected: `fabric`; no alternate preset was verified. The listing's industry cross-links are discovery links, not an exclusive niche constraint. [Listing capture](../evidence/fabric-listing.json) separates overview, feature marketing and release text.

**OBSERVED / INFERRED:** The demo presents **apparel / everyday essentials** through a **campaign-led broad apparel** approach. Merchant fit and customer intent are inferred from catalog/content; demo pricing is not proof of the merchants who will succeed with it. The catalog-size assumptions below concern the inspected sample, not a platform limit.

Large stacked gallery and adjacent compact purchase rail; named size radios and separate fit/material content. Observed option-group text: Size / XXS / XS / S / M / L / XL / XXL. Product-image alt text and available stock labels are content, not validated certification. On narrow screens the sequence must be judged from [mobile product evidence](../evidence/fabric-product-mobile.jpg), not an editor's device frame. Gallery/detail ordering in that capture is the observed state; sticky behavior during arbitrary scroll positions is not exhaustively verified.

Captured empty-cart entry. No purchase, checkout commitment or order was made. Empty-cart presentation is established; successful cart mutation, discount application, checkout, payment and fulfillment are **untested**. Related-product regions may load after the first capture; a visible placeholder is not proof recommendations completed.

## Visual system and measurement

Geist sans; near-white merchandise zones; oversized branding and edge-to-edge media. Computed body font declaration: `Geist, sans-serif`; first substantial heading declaration: `Geist, sans-serif`. These are computed CSS family lists, not a network/font-file audit. Recorded body background: `rgb(255, 255, 255)`. [Geometry/style JSON](../evidence/fabric-home-desktop.json) and, where present, product role measurements preserve individual sizes, spacing, borders and radii. A page background is not an entire semantic palette.

Actual comparison conditions: desktop **1440 × 1000 CSS px**, mobile **390 × 844**, DPR **1**. Native storefront viewports were used for final captures. No claim is made that a screenshot establishes keyboard, screen-reader, reduced-motion or performance compliance. Hover/focus and all timing stages remain incompletely tested.

## Tradeoffs and transferable lessons

Campaign scale can overshadow category labels; video and a large image sequence need bandwidth and motion controls. This is an expert hypothesis about fit/friction, not demonstrated conversion impact. The opening screenshot may show images still loading; compare mobile and role measurements before treating a blank frame as intended design.

**LetBuyy implication:** Transfer compact category navigation and fit context; replace brand spectacle with a purposeful outfit-to-size path. Shared add-to-cart, stock and hosted checkout contracts remain shared. Borrow a task-support principle; do not reproduce Shopify assets, copy, private behavior or an entire composition. Listed app/B2B capabilities are not automatically present in LetBuyy.
