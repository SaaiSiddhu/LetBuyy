Research: 22 October 2026. This is a comparative expert inspection, not user testing or a certification.

**DOCUMENTED:** [Official listing](https://themes.shopify.com/themes/craft/presets/craft); developer Shopify; free catalog entry; version 16.1.0, dated 9 October 2026. No claim is made that a screenshot establishes keyboard, screen-reader, reduced-motion or performance compliance. Hover/focus and all timing stages remain incompletely tested.

## Tradeoffs and transferable lessons

Material storytelling cannot replace numeric size; bundles here do not prove automatic inventory composition. This is an expert hypothesis about fit/friction, not demonstrated conversion impact. The opening screenshot may show images still loading; compare mobile and role measurements before treating a blank frame as intended design.

**LetBuyy implication:** Transfer material/care support and room/category collage; make measurement and delivery constraints explicit for furniture. Shared add-to-cart, stock and hosted checkout contracts remain shared. Borrow a task-support principle; do not reproduce Shopify assets, copy, private behavior or an entire composition. Listed app/B2B capabilities are not automatically present in LetBuyy.
