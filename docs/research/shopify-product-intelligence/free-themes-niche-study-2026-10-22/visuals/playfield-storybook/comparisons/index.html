User problem and retained convention

The adult buyer needs a factual sense of the object. Keep whole-product media, named selection and explicit dimensions. The observed reference separates dimensions into compact factual tiles.

Original departure and required contract

Playfield emphasizes 12 tiles + 2 cards + 1 tray, with merchant age and warning reachable at selection. A schematic has the same units as its text. Class C toy facts/public projection and class A SKU/cart controls must be wired before use.

User problem and retained convention

Unfamiliar products need understandable entry points. Retain category pathways and explanatory product context. Crave's observed opening is food-oriented; its theme name is not evidence of a toys niche.

Original departure and required contract

Playfield starts with adult age and interest, then age groups and contents-rich cards. Class C bounded AND facets need real query/pagination and missing-age semantics. The local fixture demonstrates clear-one without erasing the other filter.

Interpretations are design hypotheses. No purchase was made on the reference stores. Nonempty-cart, checkout completion, all filter combinations, keyboard and performance behavior are untested in these comparisons. Some lower Crave media is unloaded in saved evidence; blanks are not inferred design choices.
