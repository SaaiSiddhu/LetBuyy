Why the middle structure matters

MadeYours creates a reviewed specification. Kindred helps select a ready-made assortment; Threadline helps judge fit and material. Their reliable cart/account mechanics can remain shared.

The difference persists with every product image removed: a validated text value, explicit production constraints and immutable item-level configuration are central data and interaction structures.

The gifts/personalization boundary remains explicit. No gift note, artwork upload, proof approval or new fulfillment service is introduced by this design. Comparison is a design heuristic; it does not claim usability outcomes.
