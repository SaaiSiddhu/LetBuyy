Visual-only source / design v1

MadeYours responsive story.

15 connected scenes. Home and PDP continuation states share their intended production route. No API, identity, order or payment mutation is implemented.

01-home-top · A considered base. Your words.

Process-led opening, original product still life, choose CTA and visible three-stage journey.

02-home-bottom · Two bases. Many meanings.

Base merchandising, examples and limitations, production care and footer.

03-collection · Choose your base

Meaningful base cards, search, method and price facets, sort and honest single-page pagination.

04-product-main · First, choose your base.

Gallery, INR price, real named fixture variant and unavailable alternative; configuration-first purchase entry.

05-product-specify · Make space for your words.

Bounded text workspace with retained invalid input, precise error, last-valid illustrative preview and correction path.

06-product-review · Check it. Make it yours.

Exact authoritative review, deliberate acceptance, policy details and independent notebook link.

07-cart · Your words, kept separate.

Two configurations of the same walnut SKU, per-line editing/quantity/removal, preserved edit on failure, correct subtotal.

08-checkout-address · Where should it go?

Canonical address step, contact/phone validation, shipping quote and exact configured summary.

09-checkout-review · One last look.

Full configured summary, address and eligible mock method; uncertain result recovery checks original attempt.

10-sign-in · Sign in to LetBuyy

Canonical LetBuyy email/password entry, configured bot-check boundary and explicit separate continuation.

11-sign-up · Create your LetBuyy account

Canonical registration with minimum-eight password and linked explicit signed-in Continue to MadeYours state.

12-profile · Hello, Asha.

Store-scoped profile overview, supported name edit and useful account navigation.

13-orders · Your MadeYours orders.

Store-scoped order history, configured-item context, status and direct owned order entry.

14-order-detail · The words you ordered.

Owned immutable item configuration, same-SKU distinction, totals and merchant-received snapshot plus bounded production status.

15-addresses · Your saved places.

Default address and edit form with recoverable pincode validation, uncommitted changes and default choice.
