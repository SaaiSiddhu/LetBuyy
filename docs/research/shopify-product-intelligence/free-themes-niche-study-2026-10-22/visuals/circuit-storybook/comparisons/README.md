# Circuit comparison evidence

`index.html` is the research side-by-side review. Its Shopify screenshots are linked saved research evidence, not theme assets. `neutral-structures.html` is an original abstract electronics/books hierarchy comparison.

`*-neutral.png` are original Circuit source renders with branding/color/photography suppressed. `specs-mobile-detail.png` and `model-reference-mobile-detail.png` are native-width element captures for reading the table; the 45 required exports remain full-page and unchanged.

`gallery-*.png`, `neutral-structures-review.png`, `hindi-product-mobile.png` and `recovery-states-mobile.png` are supplementary QA evidence, excluded from the 45/15 counts.

`sample-*` files are the preflight capture pass. Final images are only in `../images/`; final boards are only in `../boards/`. Sample audit records remain as provenance and are not the final export manifest.
