Inside the book

A city, read
at walking pace.

Twenty-four short essays about paying attention to the ordinary: a tree beside a gate, the sound of an evening market, a street taken slowly.

This collection brings together notes on city life, memory and the small habits that change the way a place feels.

Read a little · From the opening essay

The long way home

The first thing I noticed was the tree. It had stood beside the doorway for years, but that morning its shadow crossed the pavement differently. I stopped, and the street stopped being a route.

A city has more than one map. There is the map of where we need to be, and the quieter map of what we have begun to notice. I had spent a long time following the first.

That day I took the long way home. Nothing remarkable happened. I remembered it anyway.
