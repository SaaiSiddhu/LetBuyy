Production config declares the hosted checkout origin:
66:CHECKOUT_ORIGIN = "https://checkout-staging.letbuyy.com"
157:CHECKOUT_ORIGIN = "https://checkout-staging.letbuyy.com"
286:CHECKOUT_ORIGIN = "https://checkout.letbuyy.com"

NOTE: 'accounts' IS reserved (the identity issuer host, LETBUYY_IDENTITY_ISSUER=
https://accounts.letbuyy.com). 'checkout' is not, despite CHECKOUT_ORIGIN naming it.
=> A merchant can claim checkout.letbuyy.com as a storefront subdomain, taking the
   hostname the Constitution designates for the ART-14 hosted checkout origin.
   Severity S2 (adversarially corrected from S1). Fix: add 'checkout' to
   RESERVED_STOREFRONT_LABELS. One line.
