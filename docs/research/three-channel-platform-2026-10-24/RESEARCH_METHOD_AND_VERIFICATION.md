# Research method counting rules and verification

## What the research counts mean

The final register contains **534 qualifying distinct external primary-source pages/documents**, across 14 subject categories. The findings register contains **580 individual implementation requirements**, across 23 domains, each with an observable acceptance condition. These meet the owner's requested minimums using different units: a source is a retrieved document, while a requirement is a proposed or confirmed behavior. They are not presented as 580 independently verified competitor facts.

The source library covers current seller/storefront models, partner disclosures, payments, India applicability, identity/security, catalog/inventory, location, cloud, native applications, accessibility and operations. It includes hundreds of technical implementation references because the requested outcome covers the complete shared platform. It does not mean 534 companies, franchise contracts, searches, legal opinions or private rate cards were available.

## Qualification and exclusions

A counted source must be a first-party publisher page/document with successful substantive text retrieval, at least 1,000 readable characters after extraction, a recorded URL, retrieval time and text hash. Canonical URL normalization and readable-content hashes remove duplicates. One 690-page PDF counts as one source, not 690. URL fragments and translated copies do not add sources.

The initial broad retrieval collected 546 candidates. Topic screening and a second targeted pass replaced irrelevant navigation, testimonials, unrelated corporate/ESG content, foreign-market or unused product material, legacy tutorials and thin pages. The final stricter threshold removed additional short reference/index pages even where they were potentially useful. The source register retains exclusions and failed-attempt history so an unavailable response is not silently counted as research evidence.

Three important partner landing pages were inspected through indexed first-party text but blocked or challenged direct retrieval. They are explicitly listed as supplemental evidence and excluded from the 534 qualifying full-text count. Failed or insufficient PDF extraction is likewise excluded. A failed earlier request can coexist with a later successful retrieval in the attempt history.

## Depth and claim support

Broad sources were retrieved, extracted and screened for publisher/topic relevance. Material commercial, legal, security and architectural claims received focused reading of the relevant passages. SOURCE_REGISTER.json distinguishes focused claim reading from broader topic screening. This is not a line-by-line reading of every source, every prospectus page or every repository file.

The primary-source citations beside narrative claims establish the relevant facts. The source catalog also maps documents to potentially related requirements as a navigation aid. Those topic matches are not independent verification that a source proves the entire requirement, that a competitor uses the proposed design, or that LetBuyy already implements it.

The 580 requirements express owner direction and source-informed engineering design. Each has an acceptance outcome, named ownership area, budget bucket and local design volume. A missing external context link is intentional for requirements grounded in owner decisions or local architectural rules. It is not repaired by attaching an unrelated citation.

## Current source versus historical evidence

SOURCE_OBSERVATIONS.json pins relevant working-file observations, including the earlier source audit and added Seller/identity/channel evidence. The checkout has substantial pre-existing changes. A Git HEAD alone cannot identify that working state; the file hashes preserve the inspected inputs.

Important distinctions are retained: the Seller and Store Ops trees are preserved reference source; Delivery is deferred; the principal/account-link foundation has shadow/future-cutover code; native prepaid is not established by an SDK dependency; category snapshots do not prove fee selection; donor stock/cash/payment helpers are not canonical authority. Earlier test reports are historical evidence, not freshly executed acceptance.

## Financial assumptions and legal timing

FINANCIAL_SCENARIOS.csv contains invented INR ex-tax Quick inputs with explicit formulas in Volume 7. It is not a competitor rate card, vendor quote, signed franchise offer or investment return forecast. All five cases are independently recalculated in document verification, including the negative-contribution case with no finite positive break-even.

Public provider/cloud prices are dated capabilities and units, not account eligibility or LetBuyy's actual bill. Legal statements distinguish present rules from staged or future commencement. The controlling instrument, actual entity/category applicability and professionally accepted configuration remain release evidence where required.

## Scope of execution

Only this research chat was resumed by direct human instruction. RESEARCH_RESUME_SCOPE.json records that boundary. The shared project pause record and other chats are unchanged. No new chat, subagent, coordination message or automation was started for this research.

The work writes research documents and machine-readable research artifacts, plus a supersession notice in the earlier Quick plan. It performs public read-only research and source inspection. It does not implement apps, activate reference/deferred roots, modify database schema, deploy, provision paid infrastructure, change private cloud resources, send franchise offers, charge users or perform runtime acceptance tests.

## Document verification

VERIFICATION.json records exact counts and file hashes after checks for source/requirement uniqueness, source qualification and cross-references, source observation consistency, Markdown/table/fence structure, local file links and line bounds, internal document/anchor links, financial scenario arithmetic, roadmap day totals and dependency cycles.

The dependency record splits P7a, the early commercial/snapshot prerequisites, from P7 closure. P7a consumes no additional budget because it is part of P7. This avoids a false circular schedule in which checkout needs fee contracts but fee contracts wait for completed checkout.

Mermaid diagrams are checked as source text for declarations and structure. Pixel rendering is not certified without a renderer. Runtime builds, DB/security suites, device flows, provider sandbox, hosted resources and release gates are **not executed by this research**. Their future acceptance requirements are specified rather than marked green.

## Handoff

The research package is complete when the document checks pass and the requested sources, requirements, dedicated-app architecture, product reuse, channel charges, partner models, identity, branding, financial scenarios and implementation gates are present. The next implementation task is P0 only after a later implementation instruction. No research completion statement resumes the other paused LetBuyy work.

The owner subsequently added Marketplace web and Store Ops web. Volume 16 records the additional frontend scope, 30 added requirements, three extra budget buckets and revised full-scope estimate. Source corpus qualification remains 534 documents.
