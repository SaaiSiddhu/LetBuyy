# Market research and competitor operating models

Research date: 24 October 2026. The source catalog is a broad technical and commercial reference library. It is not a claim that hundreds of different franchise contracts or private seller rate cards were available.

## What the current evidence changes

Amazon India's public rate card includes category and price-dependent referral rates, including eligible zero-rate products, alongside separate closing, shipping and other service charges. A category column alone therefore cannot reproduce a mature fee system. LetBuyy needs its own versioned schedule and complete proceeds preview, rather than copying an old percentage table. [Amazon fees and pricing](https://sell.amazon.in/fees-and-pricing).

Flipkart's November 2025 announcement introduced zero commission for eligible sub-₹1,000 products. Its developer documentation distinguishes listing and order operations, including hyperlocal listing contracts. These support separate seller workflows and explicit offer/fulfillment data, not a requirement to copy Flipkart's private implementation. [Official announcement](https://stories.flipkart.com/announcement/flipkart-launches-zero-commission-model-for-products-below-rs-1-000-strengthens-seller-success-with-unmatched-supply-chain-reliability-and-technology), [Seller platform overview](https://seller.flipkart.com/api-docs/FMPlatOverview.html).

These observations mean “Amazon/Flipkart-like” should describe seller enrollment, catalog offers, order operations, statements and trust controls. It must not imply that every current competitor order pays a positive category commission, or that LetBuyy must adopt their exact economics.

## Comparison and adoption decisions

| Reference model | Relevant observable pattern | LetBuyy implication | Do not infer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Amazon Seller | Seller registration, dedicated seller tools, existing-catalog versus new-product paths, differentiated fulfillment | Dedicated Seller app and explicit product/listing identity | Private eligibility, guaranteed payout timing or a universal commission |
| Flipkart Seller | Listing/order APIs, seller operations and current zero-commission programme | Separate app experience, accurate rate versions, operational state machines | Its public API version is LetBuyy's implementation or current private contract |
| Shopify | Product publication, physical locations, inventory and local delivery/routing concepts | One merchant catalog with channel offers and location stock | Shopify Payments availability or fee policy applies in India to this project |
| WooCommerce | Distinct product, order and storefront management concepts | Storefront remains merchant-controlled and independently useful | A separate per-merchant database is required |
| Blinkit Partners | Operator role, staff/inventory operations and turnover-linked partner compensation | Compare managed-operator economics separately from inventory-owning franchisees | A public marketing ROI is a guaranteed investment return |
| Zepto Growth Partners | Store operations, partner assistance and payouts; prospectus contract disclosures | Distinguish association rights, deposits, operating compensation and merchandise seller | Unofficial franchise-cost websites provide an approved price |
| Instamart | Public seller, franchise and property-partner entry points | Product supplier, store operator and landlord are different relationships | Every partnership has the same ownership or fee stack |

Shopify's location and local-delivery documentation provides practical reference behavior for routing and fulfillment. The relevant transfer is explicit inventory location and service promise, not replicating its entire platform. [Inventory and locations](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/inventory-and-locations), [Local delivery](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/setup/delivery-methods/local-delivery).

## Dark-store partnership evidence

Blinkit's official indexed partner page describes operational responsibility and monthly turnover-linked compensation. Its public application is an operating partnership, not evidence that an operator owns all merchandise. No contractual rupee fee was verified from the inspected partner material. [Blinkit Partners](https://partners.blinkit.com/).

Zepto's 2026 updated draft prospectus describes Growth Partner arrangements involving a refundable security deposit and one-time brand association fee, with compensation paid for operating services. That model contains flows in both directions. It does not substantiate a universal amount or justify charging LetBuyy's inventory-owning franchisee the same stack. The document is a dated disclosure rather than a live offer to LetBuyy. [Official prospectus](https://cdn.zeptonow.com/investors/documents/Zepto%20Limited%20-%20UDRHP-I.pdf).

Instamart's public partner surface distinguishes becoming a seller, opening a franchise store and renting property. Those are three different counterparties to model. Exact investment and recurring charges remain contract facts, not numbers to fill from franchise portals. [Instamart partners](https://www.swiggy.com/instamart-partner).

The partner landing pages above were available through indexed primary-source text while some direct retrievals returned bot challenges. They are separately labelled in the evidence record and are not inflated into the qualifying full-text source count. The downloaded Zepto disclosure was searched for the relevant operating agreements; its entire 690-page prospectus was not line-by-line reviewed.

## Market implications for the three channels

Storefront should solve merchant ownership and independence first: branded site, reusable catalog, dependable stock and explainable orders without a sales commission. Marketplace should earn its category fee through discovery, trust, merchandising, order operations and provider-supported seller services. Quick should charge for a clearly described demand, fulfillment, software or brand service while proving local delivery economics.

Charging merchants for access to a channel is different from creating demand there. Seller fees do not establish customer acquisition. A supermarket's offline turnover does not establish capacity to pick online orders, and an addressable neighborhood does not establish economically viable order density. Validate acquisition cost, repeat rate, order contribution, stock accuracy and failed-delivery cost separately.

Recommended competitive positioning: “Build your own store with LetBuyy Store; enable eligible products on LetBuyy Marketplace through LetBuyy Seller; operate local Quick fulfillment through an approved partner model.” Keep optional channel participation explicit. Avoid forcing Marketplace enrollment to launch a storefront or using shared inventory as a reason to commission storefront sales.

## Questions public research cannot answer

Public documentation cannot establish LetBuyy's provider approval, negotiated fees, a particular partner's rent/staff cost, local demand, current trademark clearance or exact private franchise contract. The implementation plan names the fields, contract responsibilities and acceptance evidence required to close those points. It does not substitute a competitor anecdote for an owned pilot measurement.
