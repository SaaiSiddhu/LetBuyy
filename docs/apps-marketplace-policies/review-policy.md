# Apps Marketplace Review Policy

This scaffold mirrors the source-backed `app_review_policies` structure. Final policy wording requires legal approval before public launch.

## Built Review Checks

- Developer approval and consent status.
- Valid app manifest, slug match, known scopes, redirect URLs, app URL, and privacy URL.
- Static security scan for public URLs and function/module safety.
- Pricing plan, support SLA, data-handling disclosure, compatibility metadata, and at least one real screenshot.
- Version records and re-review when developers submit app updates.

## Review Outcomes

- Approved apps can publish to the marketplace.
- Rejected or flagged apps remain out of public catalog surfaces.
- Developers receive action notes through the review status flow.
- Review moderation actions preserve report, proof, fraud, and activity metadata.

## Appeals

Developer appeal policy and legal wording are not finalized in code. Until approved, do not show a user-facing appeal promise beyond generic support contact guidance.
