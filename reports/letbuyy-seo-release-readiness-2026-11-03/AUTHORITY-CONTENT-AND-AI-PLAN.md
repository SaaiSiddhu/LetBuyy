## Three implementation briefs

**First-store walkthrough:** retain the existing URL and topic owner. Lead with account/email/draft prerequisites, show real current screenshots for supported steps, include expected outcomes and recovery for failures, and link pricing/themes/payments/shipping. Concept illustrations must not be presented as screenshots. No fabricated live orders or customer results. Verify task completion using owned fixtures and obtain native-language review before publication.

**Pricing cost explanation:** explain 0% platform commission separately from subscription, provider and tax charges. Use gateway availability and coming-soon states; link current pricing and billing terms rather than copying permanent price tables into articles. Verify SSR, hydration and retry parity. A browser CTA is not a completed purchase.

**Fulfillment guide:** explain what to do after a supported order: stock check, packing, provider choice, tracking and return/failure recovery. Source provider constraints and mark synthetic examples. The fulfillment query's small impression count is a prioritization clue, not market-size or revenue proof.

Publishing, paid-provider credits and outreach remain separately authorized. Truthful Organization bylines and empty release-history states remain deliberate. No filler pages, word-count quotas, unsupported named experts or invented releases were added.
