Merchant Growth Journey

From opening day to your next big move.

Explore five moments in a growing business—and the LetBuyy tools you can use at each one.

Storefronts + Themes

Make it yours. Then open the doors.

Turn your products into a storefront that feels like your business. Start with the essentials and publish when you are ready.

  1. Shape your storefront

    Choose a theme, adjust your branding and preview it on different screens.

  2. Prepare your catalogue

    Add product photos, prices, sizes and available stock.

  3. Make it easy to find

    Use your LetBuyy address or connect an eligible custom domain.

Orders + Payments

Your first sale. A clear next step.

When a customer buys, bring the order, payment and delivery details together. Follow the work from purchase to a prepared parcel.

  1. Review the purchase

    See what was ordered, who bought it and where it needs to go.

  2. Follow the payment

    Check whether the order is paid, pending or cash on delivery.

  3. Prepare the parcel

    Pack the items, add shipment details and follow delivery progress.

Inventory + Orders

More orders. A more organised day.

Keep the stockroom and the order list working together. Know what is available, what needs attention and what is ready to leave.

  1. Know your stock

    Review available quantities for each product and variant.

  2. Plan a restock

    Check stock history and prepare incoming inventory or purchase orders.

  3. Keep fulfilment moving

    Follow pending orders, shipment updates and returns in your workspace.

Customers + Analytics

Give a first-time buyer a reason to return.

Get to know your customers and plan your next collection with better context. Build the relationship beyond one transaction.

  1. Understand your customers

    Find customer profiles, purchase history and useful segments.

  2. Share the next collection

    Check the supported communication tools and customer consent before planning a campaign.

  3. Learn from your sales

    Review product performance and revenue before planning your next move.

B2B + AI + Analytics

A wider business. A connected workspace.

As the business becomes more complex, bring wholesale work, AI assistance and reporting into the same operating picture.

  1. Organise wholesale work

    Discuss business-buyer and bulk-order requirements with support before relying on their availability.

  2. Work with AI assistance

    Prepare supported content drafts and review suggestions before acting.

  3. Keep the wider view

    Use your commerce reports to understand the business across its activities.

What does that mean for your day?

Start with the job you need to do.

An order comes in+

Open the order to see the items, customer, payment and delivery details. Follow its progress from preparation through delivery.

A payment needs checking+

Look at the order’s payment status and transaction details. An online payment and a cash-on-delivery order can be followed separately.

A customer needs a refund+

Review the order and refund details in your workspace. Follow the refund status through your connected payment provider rather than assuming it has completed.

A product is running low+

Check quantities for each product and variant, review stock history and prepare a restock or purchase order.

You have a new collection to share+

Create an email campaign, choose subscribed customers or a customer segment, preview the audience and review the message before sending. Live email delivery requires email setup.

You want to understand your earnings+

Review sales, payments, refunds and available settlement information. Payment-provider processing and settlement timing are separate from an order being placed.
