## Interpretation controls

- Current shared HTTP captures take precedence over older search excerpts. Current homepage ecosystem links are guides; stale search excerpts describing them as live do not prove the present page still does so.
- Search queries are English with India in some query text. Tool country/device/personalization controls and exact Google rankings were unavailable. No search volumes, PAA/ad inventory, numeric consensus or persona/SXO score is inferred.
- Actual AI-answer citation counts are not measured. Robots access, a normal web result and extractable HTML are different observations.
- Purpose-specific AI controls were checked against current Google/OpenAI primary documentation. Search, training and user-triggered browsing remain separate. No training policy was changed.
- No special AI files, FAQ rich results, WebMCP tools, UCP, Product markup, LocalBusiness markup or location-page network is recommended as a blanket ranking requirement.
- An indexed consumer Google Play listing is a brand-reconciliation candidate only; affiliation/current listing content was not verified. It is not added as a confirmed site defect.
- No backlink toxicity, total authority, competitor referring-domain gap or link-velocity claim is made from one verified candidate. Parent credential/Common Crawl measurements belong in final coverage.
