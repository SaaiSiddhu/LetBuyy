    - paragraph: Input Tax Credit (ITC) lets you deduct GST paid on business purchases from GST collected on sales. If you pay ₹18 GST on packaging materials and collect ₹60 GST on sales, you only remit ₹42. This is significant — ITC on packaging, courier services, and raw materials can reduce your effective tax rate by 30–40%.
