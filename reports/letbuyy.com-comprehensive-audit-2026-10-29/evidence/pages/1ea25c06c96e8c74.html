AI in ecommerce isn't about robots replacing your team. It's about amplifying what one merchant can do: write 100 product descriptions in the time it took to write 5, respond to 200 customer queries while you sleep, and know which products will run out before they do.

What AI Can Do for Your Store Today

  • Generate product descriptions from titles and specs
  • Write reply suggestions for customer support tickets
  • Predict which products are at risk of stockout
  • Segment customers by purchase behavior automatically
  • Translate product descriptions to regional languages
  • Generate promotional content for WhatsApp and email campaigns
  • Recommend related products at checkout based on cart contents

High-ROI AI Use Cases for Indian Merchants

Product Description Generation

Describe a product in 10 words. The AI writes a 300-word SEO-optimized description in English and Hindi, with bullet points, care instructions, and an objection-handling paragraph. What took 45 minutes takes 2.

Customer Support Automation

"Where is my order?" accounts for 65% of ecommerce support tickets. AI auto-responds with real-time tracking information — without a human in the loop.

Inventory Prediction

Based on sales velocity, seasonal patterns, and upcoming campaigns, the AI flags which products will stockout before your next reorder can arrive.

India-Specific AI Context

LetBuyy's AI Copilot is trained on India-specific ecommerce data: Indian customer support patterns, GST workflows, COD logistics flows, and Indian-language product content.
