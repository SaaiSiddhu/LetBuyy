  - text: 1.9.0 22 Jul 2026 feature
  - heading "Theme marketplace opens to partner submissions" [level=3]
  - text: Design partners can now submit themes for review from the developer portal Every submission runs an automated safety check before it reaches a live store Theme previews now render your own products instead of placeholder samples Switching themes keeps your existing section settings where the layouts match 1.8.1 9 Jul 2026 fix
  - heading "Checkout and GST invoice fixes" [level=3]
  - text: Inter-state orders now show IGST correctly instead of splitting into CGST and SGST Double-tapping Place Order on a slow connection no longer creates a duplicate order COD confirmation messages now send reliably to numbers saved with a +91 prefix The GST invoice PDF is now attached to the order confirmation email automatically 1.8.0 24 Jun 2026 feature
  - heading "Gift cards and store credit" [level=3]
  - text: Issue gift cards with a fixed value and an optional expiry date Buyers can apply a gift card at checkout alongside a coupon Refunds can be returned as store credit instead of to the original payment method Gift card balances now appear on the customer profile with full redemption history 1.7.0 5 Jun 2026 feature
  - heading "WhatsApp order updates" [level=3]
  - text: Send order confirmation, shipping and delivery updates over WhatsApp Message templates in English and Hindi, editable per store COD orders can now ask for a WhatsApp confirmation before they enter fulfilment 1.6.2 21 May 2026 security
  - heading "Account and payment hardening" [level=3]
  - text: Payment provider callbacks are now verified with a timing-safe signature check Store sign-in is rate limited to five attempts every fifteen minutes Refunds and payouts now require a second confirmation step Signing out now ends the session on every device, not just the current one 1.6.0 6 May 2026 feature
  - heading "Multi-location inventory (beta)" [level=3]
  - text: Hold stock across more than one warehouse or shop Orders route automatically to the location closest to the buyer Low stock alerts can be set per location The inventory screen now shows stock split across all your locations 1.5.0 15 Apr 2026 feature
  - heading "Custom domains" [level=3]
  - text: Connect your own domain and we handle the SSL certificate Guided DNS setup with live verification while you wait Certificates now renew on their own — nothing to remember 1.4.0 26 Mar 2026 improvement
  - heading "Faster checkout on Indian networks" [level=3]
  - text: Checkout now loads in about half the time it used to on 4G UPI apps installed on the buyer’s phone are detected and shown first Address autofill now covers PIN codes across the country Switching between UPI and COD no longer clears the address already entered 1.3.0 10 Mar 2026 feature
  - heading "Developer platform" [level=3]
  - text: API keys with per-key permissions, issued from the developer portal Webhooks for order, payment and inventory events Failed webhooks retry automatically with a widening gap between attempts A delivery log shows the last 500 attempts for each endpoint
