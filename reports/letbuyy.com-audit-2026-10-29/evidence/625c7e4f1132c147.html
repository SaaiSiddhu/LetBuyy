How Themes Work on LetBuyy

The technical and merchant guide to themes, customization, and the theme engine.

L

LetBuyy TeamPlatform

20 Apr 20269 min readUpdated 11 Jun 2026

#themes#storefront#customization#platform
Designed around your business

Your theme is your storefront's design layer. It controls layout, typography, colors, animations, and how products and collections are displayed. LetBuyy themes come in three flavors: free, marketplace, and custom.

01Choose your foundation

Theme Types

  • Free Themes: Maintained by LetBuyy — Clarity, Urban Kicks, Minimal, Grid

  • Marketplace Themes: Built by third-party developers, reviewed by LetBuyy, paid or free

  • Custom Themes: Built by your team or an agency, uploaded directly to your store

02Make it your own

The Theme Editor

Open Storefront → Theme Editor from your dashboard. You'll see a live preview of your store on the left and customization panels on the right. Changes are previewed in real time before you save.

  • Colors: Primary, secondary, text, background, border colors
  • Typography: Font family, sizes, and weights per element
  • Sections: Reorder, hide, and configure homepage sections
  • Header/Footer: Navigation, logo, social links
  • Product Cards: Layout, image aspect ratio, info shown
03Build your pages

Every theme page is built from sections — modular blocks that can be reordered, duplicated, and configured. Common sections: Hero image, Product grid, Testimonials, Image with text, Video embed, Newsletter signup, FAQs.

04Bring it to life

Publishing Changes

  1. DraftOnly you
  2. PreviewYour team
  3. PublishedYour visitors

Theme changes have three states: Draft (only you see it), Preview (share via preview link for team review), and Published (live for all visitors). Always preview before publishing.

Theme Backups

Before any major theme change, duplicate your theme as a backup (Theme Actions → Duplicate). If something breaks, swap back to the backup in one click.
