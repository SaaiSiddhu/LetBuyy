Step 1: Decide What You're Selling

Before touching a single platform, clarify your product-market fit. The best-performing Indian D2C stores in 2026 fall into three buckets: high-margin branded goods (fashion, beauty, supplements), regionally-authentic products (handlooms, artisan crafts, regional foods), and business supplies (stationery, packaging, tools).

Key Insight

Undersaturated categories win in Tier 2 India

Categories that are oversaturated globally — like candles, phone cases, or basic apparel — often have 5–10 years of real runway in Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities where organized ecommerce barely exists. The competition you're benchmarking against is local offline retail, not Amazon.

Step 2: Get Your Legal and GST Setup Right

You need a GSTIN before you can sell on most platforms or accept online payments at scale. Registration takes 7–10 working days through the GST portal. You'll need your PAN, Aadhaar, bank account details, and a business address.

GST Registration Checklist

PAN card of the business owner or entity

Aadhaar card for identity verification

Bank account statement or cancelled cheque

Proof of business address (rent agreement or utility bill)

Digital signature certificate (for company/LLP)

MOA/AOA if registering a Private Limited company

  • Sole proprietor: No separate registration needed. GST under your PAN.
  • Partnership / LLP: Register with MCA first, then GST.
  • Private Limited: Recommended for brands expecting VC or large scale.

Don't Skip This

If your annual turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh in special category states), GST registration is mandatory. Even below that, voluntary registration unlocks input tax credit — a significant cost advantage.

Step 5: Configure Payments

Indian buyers have strong payment preferences that differ significantly from Western markets. In 2026, UPI accounts for 65% of online transactions, COD remains at 30% for Tier 2+ cities, and cards cover the remainder.

COD Strategy

Don't view COD as a burden — view it as your Tier 2 and Tier 3 city unlock. Brands that disable COD lose up to 40% of addressable revenue in India. Offer it, but set a ₹2,000 order minimum to manage return risk.

Step 6: Sort Your Logistics

Shipping is where most new Indian stores fail silently. Choose a courier partner with strong Tier 2 and Tier 3 coverage — not just metro cities. Look for NDR (non-delivery report) automation, real-time tracking, and RTO (return to origin) rates under 15%.

AI Recommendation

How to choose the right courier partner for your stage

Under ₹50K monthly GMV: Use Shiprocket. Aggregates 17+ couriers, no volume minimums, simple dashboard.
₹50K–₹5L GMV: Negotiate directly with Delhivery. Better per-shipment rates and Tier 2 coverage than aggregators.
₹5L+ GMV: Split across 2 couriers. Delhivery for volume + BlueDart for high-value or time-sensitive orders.
RTO above 20%: This is a marketing problem, not a logistics problem. Tighten your targeting before scaling.
  • Shiprocket: Best aggregator for small volumes (<500 shipments/month)
  • Delhivery: Strong for Tier 2/3 coverage, enterprise pricing starts lower
  • BlueDart: Premium, metro-heavy, great for high-value goods
  • DTDC: Widest PIN code coverage in India

Step 7: Launch and Validate

Don't aim for perfection before launch. Aim for your first 10 orders. Real customer feedback is worth more than any amount of internal testing. Launch to your existing network first — WhatsApp groups, Instagram, college alumni networks — before spending on ads.

Pre-Launch Checklist

Test checkout end-to-end on mobile (iPhone and Android)

Place a real test order with UPI and verify GST invoice

Check that all product images load fast on 4G

Set up WhatsApp Business number for support

Write 5 WhatsApp messages to send to your first potential buyers

Announce on Instagram Story with swipe-up link to store

First 10 Orders Milestone

Getting 10 orders from people who don't know you personally is the single most important validation milestone for any Indian D2C brand. Everything before that is planning. Everything after that is execution.
