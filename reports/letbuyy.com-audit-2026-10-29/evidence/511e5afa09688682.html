LetBuyy is not a website builder. It's a commerce operating system — a connected set of surfaces that merchants, developers, operators, and customers interact with simultaneously.

The Core Surfaces

  • Merchant Control Plane (merchant.letbuyy.com): Where you run your store — products, orders, customers, inventory, analytics, settings
  • Storefront Runtime ({store}.letbuyy.com): Your live store, powered by your theme and products
  • Identity Platform (accounts.letbuyy.com): Authentication, onboarding, plan management
  • App Marketplace (apps.letbuyy.com): Install third-party extensions
  • Theme Marketplace (themes.letbuyy.com): Browse and purchase themes
  • Developer Portal (developer.letbuyy.com): API docs, webhook management, app creation

The Architecture in Plain Language

Every LetBuyy store is identified by a site_id. All data (products, orders, customers, inventory) is scoped to your site_id. When a buyer visits your store, the storefront runtime uses your site_id to look up your configuration, theme, and products. When a merchant logs in, their session is scoped to the store(s) they own.

India-First Decisions in the Platform

  • Money is stored as minor units (paise) to avoid floating-point errors
  • GST invoices are generated automatically for every order
  • UPI and COD are first-class citizens in the checkout flow
  • All currency formatting defaults to INR with en-IN locale
  • Address forms include GST-aware state codes
  • Storefront routing supports custom domains with instant SSL

2026 Roadmap Highlights

Product launches planned or shipping in 2026: Multi-language storefronts (Hindi, Tamil, Bengali), B2B selling mode with invoice ordering, WhatsApp native checkout, advanced AI Copilot with voice input, and merchant mobile app.
