# Search experience and intent

Dominant site type: B2B commerce SaaS / online store builder marketed to India. This is not a merchant product catalog, local shop or location-page network. Product feed/UCP/GBP/Maps recommendations are therefore not applicable from the available evidence.

A focused web-search query, `online store builder India UPI GST zero commission`, produced official commercial pages from [D2 Commerce OS](https://os.d2c.in/features/), [Storzu](https://storzu.com/), [CartWhizz](https://cartwhizz.com/online-store-builder-india), [SmolBusiness](https://smolbusiness.in/) and [Timewheel EZShop](https://timewheel.co.in/products/ezshop). These five page excerpts emphasize building an Indian online store, payment setup and offer clarity. The observed landing-page type fits LetBuyy; no page-type mismatch is established. This is a search-tool sample, not a controlled India Google top-10 study, verified ranking list or keyword-demand estimate.

User needs inferred from those signals:

- New seller, consideration: understand whether this makes an online store with UPI/COD/GST. Make the category explicit in the first screen (SEO-08).
- Price-sensitive seller, decision: understand free plan versus trial versus subscription and provider fees. Resolve the current contradiction before content expansion (SEO-03/05).
- Existing operator, decision: understand which capabilities are live, require setup, or are coming soon. Replace implied availability with accurate status and linked documentation (SEO-03).

No numeric SXO/persona score is issued because controlled SERP, search queries, customer interviews and conversion data are unavailable. A sensible content map is the existing homepage for store-builder intent, pricing for costs, existing setup guide for launch steps, and existing UPI/COD/GST guides for operational questions. Strengthen those canonical pages before creating more pages.
