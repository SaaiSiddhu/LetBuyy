GST Planning for Ecommerce Sellers in India

A checklist for checking registration, tax classification, records and filing obligations.

Check registration before accepting orders

Record your goods or services, aggregate turnover, state or union territory, interstate activity and sales channels. Do not assume that every ecommerce seller has the same registration threshold. Notification 34/2023 describes a conditional exemption for certain goods suppliers through ecommerce operators; confirm all conditions and current amendments with your adviser.

Keep invoice and return records

Use your applicable invoice requirements and filing scheme to prepare records, then check current due dates on the GST portal. Input tax credit, e-invoicing and return frequency have conditions; do not treat a software-generated invoice or a fixed monthly date as proof that your obligations are satisfied.

GST obligations depend on what you supply, where you operate and how you sell. Use this checklist to gather facts and check the applicable rules; it is not a complete tax determination for your business.

Check registration before accepting orders

Record your goods or services, aggregate turnover, state or union territory, interstate activity and sales channels. Do not assume that every ecommerce seller has the same registration threshold. Notification 34/2023 describes a conditional exemption for certain goods suppliers through ecommerce operators; confirm all conditions and current amendments with your adviser.

  • GST notification 34/2023 — conditions and commencement

Verify classification and the applicable rate

Identify the correct HSN or SAC classification, product description, value and effective supply date. Look up the applicable notification and amendments instead of using a generic apparel, footwear or electronics rate table. Retain the source used for each product's tax configuration.

  • GST portal — current taxpayer services

Check marketplace TCS separately

Establish whether the selling arrangement involves an ecommerce operator required to collect TCS. The government documented a TCS rate reduction in 2024, so an old blanket 1% instruction should not be reused. Verify the currently applicable rate, taxable base and reconciliation process for your arrangement using official records.

  • Ministry of Finance — 2024 TCS rate change
